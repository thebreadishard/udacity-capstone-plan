# Module 02 — the opponent atlas: report

*Plan 05 (Δ-probed IR pipeline) · Udacity "AI Programming Foundations" project · first version
2026-09-10 (data obtained and parsed the same day; the notebook and this report are the deliverable,
the tables in `out/` the artefact).*

**This table is parsed from the public NASA Ames PAHdb v4.00 computed library (DOI
10.3847/1538-4365/ae1c38). It is computed science data, not AI-generated, and it is the *opponent* of
this project's pipeline, not its training data.**

## 1. Question

Make one tidy, queryable table of the computed PAH infrared band positions and intensities that the
pipeline will be compared against, and map what is in it: how many species, of what size and
charge, at which level of theory and with which scale factors; which rungs of the plan's ladder
have an entry in which comparison line; and whether the largest target class (C₃₈₄H₄₈) exists in
the library at all (frozen-lines debt 6). No model is trained; this is exploratory data analysis
with pandas, NumPy and matplotlib.

## 3. Processing

1. **Parse.** `build_opponent_atlas.py` streams the XML (`iterparse`), strips namespaces, and writes
   one row per species and one row per transition. Schema taken from NASA's own
   AmesPAHdbPythonSuite parser and its test file. Unknown scalar tags become columns; the run prints
   its tag inventory (the 4.00 file carries `n_ch` and `n_ch3` beside the documented `n_chx`).
2. **Clean.** The `formula` field carries the charge as a suffix (`C54H18-`, `C384H48+2`; 6,270 of
   10,749 species) — element counts are parsed from letters and digits only, and the suffix agrees
   with the `charge` field in every case. `uid` is unique; no non-positive frequency, no negative
   intensity. Same formula and charge with different uids are **isomers, not duplicates**: 1,159
   (formula, charge) groups, 638 with more than one uid, the largest (C₃₃H₁₉, neutral) with 358.
   Missing: the `dois` column for 3,588 species (their reference text has no DOI), `n_chx` for all
   (renamed in 4.00). The basis set is only in the Gaussian route comment; parsed for 10,717 species,
   32 carry `chkbas` (basis from a checkpoint; Mg/Fe/Si complexes, one BP86 run).
3. **Scaled vs unscaled.** `<frequency scale="s">` stores the *scaled* value; the table keeps both
   the stored value and `frequency / s`.
4. **Family label.** A frequency-range rule printed in the parser (a pilot-note candidate), used
   only to group the exploration; not an assignment.
5. **Lines C and the cheap line.** `build_line_c_table.py` reads the Mai spectra (1 cm⁻¹ grid,
   intensities normalised per spectrum) and extracts maxima ≥ 5 % of each spectrum's maximum,
   ≥ 3 cm⁻¹ apart; `build_cheap_line_table.py` joins the Bos SI table to PAHdb uids.

## 4. Findings (notebook `notebook/opponent_atlas_eda.ipynb`, five figures)

**Size and charge (fig. 1).** 10,749 species: 4,479 neutral, 2,162 cations, 2,868 dications,
1,231 anions, 9 trications. Most species have 20–100 carbons; 774 lie in the 101–386-carbon bin
(the number the v4.00 paper gives). **The 4-31G regime starts at n_C = 212** (14 species; the
largest 6-31G* species has 294).

**Scale factors as stored (fig. 2).** For the 10,703 6-31G* species: 0.9794 below ≈ 1,088 cm⁻¹,
0.9691 between ≈ 1,077 and 1,744, 0.9597 above ≈ 2,590; for the 4-31G species 0.9563 / 0.9523 /
0.9595; small sets carry 0.9097, 0.9908, 1.0145 and others. **None of the 2,517,399 transitions
carries the factors 0.964 / 0.979 / 0.975** that the plan's frozen-lines file quotes from the v4.00
paper. The atlas scores the library as served; the paper is re-read against its text before the
plan's pilot note.

**Band positions (fig. 3).** Intensity-weighted, the neutral library shows the PAH pattern: the
7.7 µm C–C/C–H in-plane region carries the most summed intensity (2.08 × 10⁶ km/mol), then the
C–H stretch (1.90 × 10⁶), the C–H out-of-plane (1.58 × 10⁶), the 8.6 µm bend (1.57 × 10⁶) and the
6.2 µm C–C stretch (1.21 × 10⁶).

**Ladder coverage (fig. 4; species counts, all charges).**

| line | R0 benzene | R1 naphthalene | R2 pyrene | R2 C₁₈H₁₂ | R3 coronene | 54–216 C | ≥ 300 C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: PAHdb theoretical 4.00 | **0** | 4 | 7 | 16 | 5 | 5,110 | 2 |
| B: PAHdb anharmonic 1.00 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 (tetracene) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| C: Mai 2025 MLMD | 0 | 1 | 2 | 7 | 1 | 388 | 0 |
| cheap: Bos 2025 SI | 0 | 1 | 3 | 6 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| lab: PAHdb experimental 3.10 (matrix) | 0 | 1 | 3 | 6 | 2 | 0 | 0 |

**Benzene is in no library except the anharmonic one.** That is consistent with the plan: benzene
is an agreement rung (decision 28) where the comparison is printed and claims nothing. Line B's
ladder coverage is benzene, naphthalene, pyrene and tetracene; chrysene, triphenylene and coronene
are compared against lines A and C and the cheap line only. Line C is a positions-only line (its
spectra are normalised), so the plan's intensity comparison has lines A and B only.

**The largest species (fig. 5; debt 6).** **C₃₈₄H₄₈ is in v4.00**: uid 617 (neutral, state label
`1-A1G`, B3LYP/4-31G, 1,290 modes, 230 with I > 1 km/mol; strongest sticks 913 cm⁻¹ at 685 km/mol
and the C–H stretch pair at 3072/3057 cm⁻¹) and uid 4447 (dication). No other species between 300
and 400 carbons. Line A is the only prediction at that size; line C stops at C₂₁₆H₃₆.

**The other two lines, as read.** Mai 2025: 1,705 species (1,704 PAHdb uids + the 49-species
experimental set), 412,938 maxima. Bos 2025: the SI's ML-scaled table covers **81 species** (n_C
10–50; the paper's "almost all of the 4000+ compounds" is not what the SI holds), 6,591 bands, the
authors' own B3LYP/4-31G frequencies with a uniform conventional factor 0.962 and the SVR value
beside them (SVR − conventional: mean −0.4 cm⁻¹, mean absolute 3.9, range −18 to +21); its 465
training instances carry no species identifier.

## 5. Limitations

- The family label is a frequency-range rule; a proper assignment needs the mode vectors, which the
  XML does not carry.
- The basis set is inferred from a free-text route line; 32 species stay unresolved.
- The symmetry-unique local-environment count for C₃₈₄H₄₈ (what the fragment-probed R6 needs) is a
  follow-up on the geometry block, not done here.
- The scale-factor discrepancy between file and paper is recorded, not explained.
