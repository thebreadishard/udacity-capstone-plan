## 1. The task, and why it is the project's own generative problem

Module 06's original idea (Capstone mapping, 6 September) was a generative model that *proposes measurement patterns*: given a molecule, which
combination of vibrational modes should the expensive calculation probe next? It was rescoped on 24 September to a SMILES generator because response
records did not exist at coupled-cluster level. On 26 September the user asked to keep the idea alive as a standout, and the missing data turned out to
exist one rung lower: the corpus's DFT-against-DFT Hessians give, for 289 molecules, the full coupling table Δ₂ and therefore the exact response of any
pattern the probe deck could ask for. Proposing the next measurement is then a simulation with no new quantum chemistry: order the deck, replay the
recovery, count energies.

The generative object is the *order* (a permutation of the deck's off-diagonal patterns), scored pattern by pattern; the generator is a scorer of pairs
of modes plus the rule "a pattern's score is the mean over the pairs it touches". Two scorers were built: P1 on hand-made pair features, P2 on a learned
molecular representation (the rung-C equivariant body of module 05, unchanged, feeding a per-mode embedding and a pair head). A VAE with an acquisition
rule (the 6 September wording) was registered as optional and not built: on 26 September a conditional scorer was the honest cheaper form, and the
combination P12 already answers whether the learned representation adds to the hand features.

## 2. Data

- Source: `../05_support_predictor/corpus/molecules/*` (layers A, A2, B; analytic Hessians of B3LYP and ωB97X at the corpus deck), exported by
  `run_export.py` to `out/exports/<id>.npz` — 289 molecules on 26 September 12:xx (index in `out/exports/index.json`).
- Per molecule: modes and frequencies, atomic participations A_ai, the low-level Hessian (the scorers' cheap side), Δ₂ = 2K/HARTREE2CM (the answer,
  for the oracle and the truth-based read-outs only), the probe deck's patterns (single, two-mode within 200 cm⁻¹, 4M multi-mode; 10 % held out) with
  their exact responses R_s = ½ aᵀ Δ a. `widen_pool()` adds a two-mode pattern for every pair for experiment E2.
- Splits (hashed, fixed before the first run): parents evaluation only (41); A2/B 70/10/20 → 171 train, 21 validation, 56 evaluation. Nothing is chosen
  on the evaluation molecules; the scorers' recipes are chosen on the 21 validation molecules only.

## 3. The recovery the simulation replays

`pp/core.py rho_curve`: consume the single-mode block, then the ordered off-diagonal patterns; after every `stride` patterns solve the banded-ℓ₁
recovery (FISTA, warm-started, λ ∈ {1e-6, 1e-5} chosen on the held-out patterns) and record (n_energies, ρ, ρ_off, Frobenius error all pairs, λ,
Frobenius error in band). n_energies counts a ± pair as two. ≈ 60 checkpoints per curve whatever the pool size. The held-out ρ_off is the plan's own
stopping quantity and needs no truth; the Frobenius columns use the truth and exist only because this is a simulation.

## 4. Read-outs and pass lines (as registered; the fallback of 12:0x was registered before any real molecule was read)

- Primary: K_off(0.3) — energies beyond the single block until ρ_off ≤ 0.3 — as a per-molecule ratio to P0 (median; fraction improved).
- Fallback where P0 does not reach 0.3 (the band pool): n_half and the AUC of ρ_off over P0's checkpoint grid, the same on the in-band Frobenius error.
- S1: P1/P0 ≤ 0.80 with ≥ 70 % improved. S2: P2 vs P1 ≤ 0.90 (the learned representation adds something). S4: the parents' P1 ratio within 0.10 of the
  A2/B ratio (transfer to size). S5 (adaptive, added 26 Sep 18:4x): P1+A better than P1 on ≥ 70 % of molecules.
- Predictions were written before each run and are scored in the outcome sections (several were wrong; the wrong ones are kept).

## 5. The learned representation gets a fair chance (the user, 26 Sep 12:1x)

No negative sentence about P2 before a five-stage search: (1) optimiser recipe, (2) loss, (3) capacity, (4) data growth at 300 molecules, (5) pretraining
on Hessian QM9. Each stage is read on the validation split by the ordering metric (Spearman ρ of predicted against true log₁₀|Δ_ij|, `stage_readout.py`),
with a margin rule written before the numbers (18:1x: the incumbent's seed spread; from stage 4: a paired per-molecule Wilcoxon test). Stages 1–3 are
read; the recipe helped a little, loss and capacity stayed within the margins with the largest model consistently ahead.

## 6. The adaptive variants (the user: "Akkoord", 26 Sep 18:4x)

P0+A, P1+A, P2+A re-rank the remaining patterns after every checkpoint by z(prior) + z(feedback), feedback being the mean over touched pairs of
log₁₀|Δ̂_ij| from the current reconstruction, with optimism (the 90th percentile of the touched pairs) for pairs no measured pattern has touched — the
plain magnitude rule never explored and lost to P0 on the planted test; the correction was registered before any real molecule ran. Purpose: split the
oracle's advantage into knowledge of the molecule and feedback during measurement.

## 7. What came out (27 September morning; details in the outcome sections and `README.md`)

1. E1 (band pool): the learned order needs ≈ one fifth of the fixed order's energies to the halfway point; oracle one sixteenth.
2. E2 (all pairs): **the pool matters more than the order** — the band deck consumed in full ends at ρ_off ≈ 0.5, a candidate set that reaches every pair
   at 0.08–0.14; ordering on that set still pays 1.3–1.4× on K_off(0.3) with P12, the oracle ×2.6–4 further. The deck change is pre-registered for after
   the 28th (`PreRegistration_2026-09-27_Wide_Candidate_Deck_Stop_Rule.md`).
3. Adaptive ordering: feedback helps the blind order (0.8×) and improves the whole curve on top of a scorer (6–10 %) but does not move the halfway point —
   the oracle gap is knowledge, not feedback. Where the knowledge comes from is registered as three tests (data, pretraining, a cheap estimate of the
   answer as an input — `PreRegistration_2026-09-27_Cheap_Proxy_Input.md`).
4. P2 at stage 0–1 does not beat P1 on the read-out; no sentence follows (§5).

## 8. Ethics and responsible use

The module proposes *which* calculation to run, never a molecule or a claim about it; its failure mode is wasted compute, not a wrong spectrum, because
the stopping quantity (held-out ρ_off) is measured, not predicted. The proxy-level nature of every number is stated wherever a number is quoted; the
oracle column exists to show the ceiling, not to be mistaken for an achievable order. All data are the project's own corpus (no personal or scraped data).
