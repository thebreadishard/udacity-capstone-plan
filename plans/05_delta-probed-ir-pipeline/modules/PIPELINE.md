# The pipeline — versions (decision 64)

The pipeline (`08_industry_synthesis/m08/pipeline.py`) takes one molecule in, as a SMILES or an xyz file, and puts its infrared spectrum
out, with a certificate (`m08/live_certificate.py`) that states how well each band is known. It carries its own version number, beside the
networks' in `MODELS.md`. A step's change raises the minor number; a change of what goes in or comes out raises the major; a new network
version is a new pipeline version; v1.0 is the version submitted with module 08. What it still needs is in
`08_industry_synthesis/PIPELINE_BACKLOG.md`; how it was built is in `08_industry_synthesis/PIPELINE_BUILD_NOTE_2026-10-29.md` (TASKS 146).

**What it takes and refuses.** One neutral, closed-shell molecule with an aromatic ring, made of the elements the networks trained on
(C, H, N, O, F, S, Cl); everything else is refused before any computation, with the reason. The certificate states the training set's
atom range, read from the checkpoints that ran: 8–30 atoms for v1.3 (pipeline 0.1–0.11), 8–40 for v1.4 (0.12); a larger molecule is
computed and its certificate says it is outside those sizes.

**What its certificate claims** (since 0.12). The correction moves B3LYP toward ωB97X/6-31G* — the DFT proxy the networks were trained
on. Under decision 66 the spectrum is made by v1.4's release networks, which trained on test sets (a) and (b) too and so have no test-set error
of their own; the error per family is that of v1.4's measurement networks, measured on molecules they never saw, in the quantity the
licence reads since 16 Nov 2026 (the corrected band positions of the seed-mean correction). Under decision 65 all four families —
CH-stretch, ring-ip, CH-oop and 'other' — are licensed (within 3 cm⁻¹ on both test sets); where a family's bootstrap interval over
molecules crosses the line, the certificate says so once. Positions are harmonic unless the anharmonic step (0.11) ran; the licence is
a statement about ω. Nothing is claimed at the coupled-cluster level. (Up to 0.11, v1.3 made and measured the spectrum, and 'other'
was not licensed.)

**Its version string.** Benzene's and phenanthrene's records were written while the code said 0.1.dev, on the same step code as 0.1
(commit 54fa017e and later; the certificates were rebuilt under decision 65); from this line on the pipeline writes 0.1.
