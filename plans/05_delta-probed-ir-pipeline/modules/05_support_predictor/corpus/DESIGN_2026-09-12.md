# Module 05 corpus — design note (2026-09-12; data design, no compute yet)

*Fixes the layers, the molecule lists, the deck and the order before the first Hessian is computed. The
size actually computed is not fixed here — the RECIPE leaves it to a dated note after the timing test
(five molecules of layer A) and the learning curve. This note may be extended by dated additions; rows
already in `manifest.csv` are never reordered or removed.*

## Why an own corpus beside Hessian QM9

Hessian QM9 (41,645 molecules, ≤ 9 heavy atoms) holds 66 molecules with an all-carbon aromatic
six-ring and 6,055 with a planar conjugated five- or six-ring (ring survey, 2026-09-12). The class
Module 05 must learn — aromatic and conjugated rings, and the size range towards the ladder molecules —
is therefore built here, with the same two functionals and basis as the plan prescribes (Δ₂ =
H(ωB97X) − H(B3LYP), 6-31G*), and Hessian QM9's conjugated subset is added as the broad small-molecule
base. All of it is computed ab initio data produced for this module; published as a release with a
DOI before the module starts (reading 1).

## Layers and priority

| layer | what | how many (manifest) | priority | Hessians computed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — size bridge** | the ladder-adjacent aromatics of 22–30 atoms: anthracene, phenanthrene, pyrene, fluoranthene, acenaphthylene, acenaphthene, fluorene, biphenylene, azulene, naphthalene and biphenyl derivatives, aza- and oxa-analogues (quinoline, isoquinoline, carbazole, dibenzofuran, dibenzothiophene, acridine, phenazine, phenanthridine), plus benzene and naphthalene themselves | explicit list in `build_manifest.py` (~45) | 1 (first) | both, at the B3LYP-optimised geometry |
| **B — the class** | mono- and di-substituted aromatic and heteroaromatic cores up to 26 atoms: cores benzene, naphthalene, pyridine, pyrimidine, pyrazine, pyrrole, furan, thiophene, imidazole, oxazole, thiazole, indole, benzofuran, benzothiophene, quinoline, isoquinoline, azulene, biphenyl, styrene; substituents CH₃, OH, NH₂, F, Cl, CN, CHO, COOH, OCH₃, NO₂, CF₃, vinyl, ethynyl, CONH₂, SH | generated, deduplicated by canonical SMILES — **4,353 in the manifest of 2026-09-12** (8–26 atoms, median 18); the hashed order makes the first 300 / 600 / 1,200 the learning-curve subsets, so the manifest may hold more than will ever be computed | 2 | both, at the B3LYP-optimised geometry |
| **C — Hessian QM9 conjugated class** | the 6,055 molecules of the ring survey's conjugated class | from `data/hessian_qm9/conjugated_ring_labels.txt` | 3 | **B3LYP only**, at the Hessian-QM9 geometry (no re-optimisation), so Δ₂ is a difference at one geometry; the ωB97X side is Hessian QM9's own (numerical, 0.01 a.u.) |

Inside a layer the order is the SHA-1 of the canonical SMILES (or of the QM9 label): fixed,
pseudo-random, machine-independent. The learning-curve subsets 300 / 600 / 1,200 of layer B are the
first 300 / 600 / 1,200 rows in that order.

## The deck (`decks/deck_v1.json`)

psi4 1.11 in the conda environment `qc` (the dry run's engine). Optimisation at B3LYP/6-31G* (layers A,
B) with psi4 defaults; analytic Hessians at B3LYP/6-31G* and ωB97X/6-31G* at that geometry; DFT grid
(75 radial, 302 angular) — **not** Hessian QM9's NWChem grid (Mura–Knowles 49/45 radial, Lebedev 434) —
because the two engines' radial schemes differ anyway; the consequence, a grid-noise floor on Δ₂ that
must be measured, is part of the timing test: the first five molecules are run twice, once with (75,
302) and once with (99, 590), and the Hessian difference is printed. Frozen core does not apply (DFT).
Translations and rotations are projected out of every stored Hessian before Δ₂ is formed. Charge 0,
singlet, closed shell. Energies in hartree, geometries in bohr in the files; the release documents units.

## Statuses and the queue

`pending` → `running` (lock + start time in the ledger) → `done` (result folder complete, hashes
written) or `failed` (psi4 error text in `result.json`; the runner moves on and never retries
automatically — a failed row is a decision for a person). A `running` row without a complete folder on
the next start is a crash: it is reset to `pending` and redone.

## The timing test (first run, after the current anchor job)

`run_corpus.py --max-molecules 5`: benzene, naphthalene, azulene, quinoline, biphenyl (the five
smallest of layer A, forced to the front for this purpose). It prints per molecule the optimisation
time, both Hessian times and the peak memory; those five numbers fix, by a dated note here, (i) the
seconds-per-molecule model that `STATUS.md` uses for its estimates, and (ii) the size actually
committed to before the module's release.

## What this note does not decide

The number of molecules computed (dated note after the timing test and the learning curve); whether
layer C is computed at all before the module starts (it is the cheapest per molecule but the largest
in count); which machine runs which layer.
