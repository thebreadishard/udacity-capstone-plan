## Status

- 2026-09-12: module scaffolded in the rubric form on branch `module-04-calibrated-harmonic`:
  RECIPE → builder → `notebook/training_table.csv` (2,477 pairs, 83 molecules; 36 % of laboratory bands
  unmatched) → `notebook/modeling.ipynb` → `module_summary.pdf`. **Result (printed by the notebook):
  no model learns the error.** Held-out MAE: zero model 6.49, per-family constant 6.43, ridge 6.40,
  gradient-boosted trees 6.48 cm⁻¹; R² ≤ 0.01; an instance-level split does not help the trees (6.65).
  The error is within-molecule scatter (SD ≈ 9 cm⁻¹), not a per-molecule offset (SD ≈ 2 cm⁻¹). By the
  RECIPE rule the baseline column is **ridge**, and it is the library as served to within 0.1 cm⁻¹.
  Uncertainty layer (ridge, held out): 68 % ≈ 7 cm⁻¹, 95 % ≈ 22 cm⁻¹ overall, per family in
  `notebook/uncertainty_layer.csv`.
- **What this means for the plan.** The proposal's "in-house calibrated-harmonic baseline" is, on this
  table, indistinguishable from line A: the three-region factors already remove the mean, and the
  descriptors carry no information about the per-band remainder. That is the P2 hypothesis' premise
  stated in the Ladder (the opponents absorb the mean; the per-band scatter is what Δ₂ must buy),
  now measured on the matrix table. Two cautions before the pilot note adopts the recipe: (i) the join
  is coarse (11.5 % of pairs beyond 15 cm⁻¹, likely mis-assignments) and inflates every model's error
  equally — a stricter join (assignment by symmetry and intensity pattern, or the mode-vector families)
  is a pilot-note amendment candidate, to be decided *before* looking at its effect on the ranking;
  (ii) the laboratory side is matrix, so this baseline reaches gas-phase scoring only through the
  Ladder §2 matrix gate (Module 03: +3.3 to +5.9 cm⁻¹ against hot vapour).
- Bos et al. 2025 for comparison (recorded in Frozen_Lines_to_Beat): MAE 10.41 → 5.07 cm⁻¹ with an
  instance-level 80/20 split from a *uniform* 0.962 start on their own B3LYP/4-31G; our zero model
  starts at 6.49 because PAHdb's three-region factors are already in the file. The numbers are not
  directly comparable (different DFT sets, join, split); the report says so.
