## Follow-up, 22 September 14:2x — xtight excludes the LNO thresholds; the geometry explains the C–H stretches

**Xtight (1e-7 / 1e-8) changes nothing:** mode 29 (a1g C–H stretch) ω′ 3173.2 → 3173.2, mode 5 (b2g out-of-plane) 680.5 → 679.5 cm⁻¹ (`R0_XTIGHT_READING_2026-09-22.md`; 9 energies, 2264 s reference, ≈ 5.5 h). LNO truncation is not the cause. **The cause is the geometry.** The deck measures every method's curvature at the B3LYP/6-31G* geometry; the literature harmonics sit at each method's own minimum, and a C–H stretch's curvature changes by tens of cm⁻¹ per hundredth of an ångström. Test with HF/cc-pVTZ, where the effect is largest (`r0_geometry_check.py`, hel1-14, 3 min): the HF a1g minimum lies at q_CH = −0.255, q_breathing = +0.733 relative to the B3LYP geometry (r_CH shorter by 0.0124 Å, energy 1,761 µE_h lower); the curvature along mode 29 rises from 3224.8 at the B3LYP geometry to **3336.2 at HF's own a1g minimum (+111.4 cm⁻¹)**. And the first-order prediction from our own B3LYP cubic constants (T3 QFF: φ₂₉,₂₉,₂₉ = −803.7, φ₂₉,₂₉,₁₂ = +31.0 cm⁻¹) times that displacement is **+113.8** — the mechanism is quantitative. For CCSD(T) the same arithmetic with r_CH shorter by 0.004–0.005 Å (the usual CC-versus-B3LYP/6-31G* difference) gives 3206–3215 against the literature 3218; the in-plane modes hardly move under this relaxation, which is why they agreed. Control: B3LYP/6-31G* with pyscf sits 0.038 in q_CH from the psi4 minimum and its curvature moves by −15.9, again as the cubics predict.

**What this means for the pipeline (proposal, not a decision):** a corrected harmonic frequency is the curvature at the low-level geometry *plus a first-order geometry term* — the high-level gradient at the low-level geometry, projected on the low-level modes, times the low-level cubic constants (Δω_i ≈ ½ Σ_k φ_iik ΔQ_k, ΔQ_k = −g_k/ω_k in reduced coordinates). The term needs one high-level gradient per molecule, not a Hessian, and the cubics come from the same two-route QFF that gives the anharmonic shifts. The corpus labels ΔH(at the B3LYP geometry) carry the same term; the ΔH model would predict the curvature part and the gradient part separately, or the label is redefined at the high-level minimum. This goes to the user for the 28 September table and as a possible decision 48; the R0 numbers in the table are presented as curvatures at the B3LYP geometry with the geometry term named.
