# Experimental references and theory-vs-experiment errors for methyl vibrations of methyl (hetero)aromatics

Scope note (4 Nov 2026): 17 tool calls; several primary papers (RSC, ACS, core.ac.uk PDFs) returned HTTP 403, so a number of values
below come from abstracts or search-result summaries rather than the full text. Each such case is marked. No experimental
fundamental is reported here that was not seen in a source this session; values recalled from memory are listed only under Gaps.

## 1. Which methyl aromatics have jet-cooled or gas-phase assigned methyl stretch / bend fundamentals?

### Takeaway
Only a few methyl aromatics have jet-cooled, assigned methyl C-H stretch spectra that are published with a theory comparison. The
best documented are 9-methylanthracene and 9,10-dimethylanthracene (IR-UV ion-dip, < 5 K, 0.07 cm-1 laser), the four
supersonic-jet molecules of Sibert et al. 2014, and toluene. Toluene's methyl stretches were measured jet-cooled only in S1, and in
S0 only in a Ne matrix, in the liquid and as gas-phase overtones. I found no jet-cooled assignment of the CH3 *bend* fundamentals
(1370-1550 cm-1) for any small methyl aromatic.

### Cited Findings
- **9-methylanthracene (MA) and 9,10-dimethylanthracene (DMA)**: supersonic Ar expansion (2 bar), IR-UV ion-dip with REMPI/TOF at
  the parent mass, internal temperature < 5 K, IR linewidth 0.07 cm-1, scanned 2750-3150 cm-1. The methyl bands are broad (>= 6 cm-1).
  Assigned alkyl bands: MA symmetric CH3 stretch 2877.5 cm-1 (strong), MA asymmetric 2933.8 cm-1; DMA symmetric 2885.2 cm-1,
  DMA asymmetric 2929.2 cm-1; a group of bands between 2917 and 2980 cm-1; a DMA band at 3031.7 cm-1 attributed to a sterically
  hindered methyl CH; alkyl absorption onset 2870 cm-1. — [Maltseva et al., "High-resolution IR absorption spectroscopy of PAHs in
  the 3 µm region: role of hydrogenation and alkylation", arXiv:1802.01497 (A&A 2018)](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1802.01497)
- **Sibert, Tabor, Kidwell, Dean, Zwier, J. Phys. Chem. A 118, 11272-11281 (2014), DOI 10.1021/jp510142g**: a local-mode
  stretch-bend Fermi-resonance Hamiltonian for CH3 and OCH3 groups, compared with jet-cooled (supersonic expansion) IR spectra of
  four molecules. I could not open the paper to name the four molecules. The abstract says the anharmonic couplings were similar from
  molecule to molecule and the harmonic terms varied more. — [Sibert group publication page](https://sibert.chem.wisc.edu/publication/fermi-resonance-effects-in-the-vibrational-spectroscopy-of-methyl-and-methoxy-groups/);
  [UW Chemistry publication list](https://chem.wisc.edu/department-publications/publications-neb-sibert/)
- **Precursor model (CH2)**: Buchanan, Dean, Zwier, Sibert, J. Chem. Phys. 2013: a cubic stretch-bend local-mode Hamiltonian with
  DFT parameters, applied to 1,2-diphenylethane and [2.2]paracyclophane. — [Sigma-Aldrich paper record](https://b2b.sigmaaldrich.com/FI/en/tech-docs/paper/310128)
- **Alkylbenzenes**: a Zwier group conference abstract reports jet-cooled, single-conformation spectra of straight-chain
  alkylbenzenes as tests of the model. — [IDEALS abstract](https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/90947)
- **Toluene in S1, jet**: UV-IR double resonance in a supersonic jet (Minejima, Ebata, Mikami, PCCP 2002, issue 9) found the methyl
  CH stretches in S1 lower in frequency than in S0. The full text returned 403, so I have no numbers from it. — [RSC landing page](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/Content/ArticleLanding/2002/CP/B108039C)
- **Toluene in S0, Ne matrix (3 K) and liquid**: Knaanie, Šebek, Tsuge, Myllys, Khriachtchev, Räsänen, Albee, Potma, Gerber,
  J. Phys. Chem. A 120, 3380-3389 (2016), DOI 10.1021/acs.jpca.6b01604. They measured the CH stretch region of toluene isotopologues
  and found the Ne-matrix spectrum more complicated than theory, attributed to matrix-site and resonance effects. The abstract gives
  no band positions. — [HUJI CRIS record](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/infrared-spectrum-of-toluene-comparison-of-anharmonic-isolated-mo/)
- **Toluene gas-phase methyl CH overtones** (C6D5CH3 and others; Cavagnat, Lespade, Lapouge): CH stretches modelled as Morse
  oscillators coupled adiabatically to the quasi-free methyl internal rotation, with strong IVR through Fermi resonances mainly
  involving the HCH bends. — [Ohio State symposium abstract](https://molspect.chemistry.ohio-state.edu/symposium_53/Abstracts/p107.html);
  [catalogue record "CH-stretching overtone spectra of a fast rotating methyl group. I. Toluene C6D5CH3"](https://acnpsearch.tweb-dev.unibo.it/singlejournalindex/80462975)
- **Toluene S0 consistent assignment**: Gardner, Green, Tamé-Reyes, Wilton, Wright, J. Chem. Phys. 2013, "Vibrations of the low
  energy states of toluene (X 1A1 and A 1B2) and the toluene cation (X+ 2B1)". It assigns the S0 fundamentals of toluene-h8 and the
  methyl-d3 isotopologue from earlier experiments plus DFT. Of the 39 modes, one is better treated as the methyl torsion. The full
  table could not be opened (403). — [Nottingham ePrints 3362](https://eprints.nottingham.ac.uk/3362/);
  [Nottingham repository](https://nottingham-repository.worktribe.com/output/714647)
- **Toluene-argon** (neutral and cation): ion-dip IR with a free-electron laser (Satink, Meijer, von Helden), with computed
  frequencies of bare toluene. — summarised in a search result; the primary record was not opened (the result list pointed to
  [lup.lub.lu.se/record/337853](https://lup.lub.lu.se/record/337853) and [fhi.mpg.de 149.pdf](https://www.fhi.mpg.de/644149/149.pdf);
  attribution not checked).

### Inferences
- Inference: no source I found gives jet-cooled, assigned **CH3 bend** fundamentals (1370-1550 cm-1) of a small methyl aromatic.
  The jet IR-UV literature on methyl groups covers almost only the 2850-3000 cm-1 region, where the bend overtones show up through
  Fermi resonance. For the bends, the realistic references are gas-phase FTIR (room temperature, band contours) and matrix isolation.
- Inference: our 1508/1521 cm-1 bands in 4/5-methylimidazole sit above the usual range of the asymmetric CH3 deformation of an
  aryl methyl. They are probably mixed ring-stretch/CH3 deformation modes, so a reference has to be judged on its *mixed* bands,
  not only on the pure CH3 deformations. Check the eigenvectors before choosing.

### Gaps
- The four molecules in Sibert et al. 2014 (I recall methyl/methoxy aromatics but did not verify; the paper must be opened).
- Jet-cooled or gas-phase assigned methyl fundamentals for o/m/p-xylene, mesitylene, the picolines, methylpyrazine,
  methylpyrimidines, the methylimidazoles, 2-methylfuran, 2-methylthiophene, methylpyrrole and 1-/2-methylnaphthalene were not found
  in this session's searches. These need dedicated searches (NIST WebBook / PNNL gas-phase spectra for band centres; Wright group
  for xylenes; Pratt/Ebata for picolines).
- Martin Suhm (jet FTIR) and Henrik Kjaergaard (local mode, gas-phase overtones of toluene/xylenes) were not reached.
- Numeric S0 methyl fundamentals of toluene (Gardner 2013 table) remain unread because of the 403.

## 2. What errors do VPT2 and other treatments show for these methyl modes? Do Fermi resonances dominate, and how large is the torsion's effect?

### Takeaway
Every source I found says the methyl C-H stretch region is shaped by stretch-bend Fermi resonance (CH3 bend overtones and
combinations), so a polyad or local-mode treatment is needed whatever is done with the torsion. Published comparisons for methyl
aromatics are qualitative or given as a percentage. I found no per-mode cm-1 error table for the methyl bends, and no published test
of VPT2 with and without the torsion decoupled.

### Cited Findings
- **MA/DMA, B3LYP/N07D, VPT2 in SPECTRO ("SP16") with resonances treated, unscaled**: in "good agreement" with the jet spectrum,
  predicting many Fermi-resonance-enabled bands. Harmonic B3LYP/N07D (scaled 0.96) predicts only two IR-active modes in the
  2750-3000 cm-1 alkyl region, which contradicts experiment. **No cm-1 errors are given for the methyl bands.** The methyl groups are
  "quasi-free rotors that so far have not been studied with our theoretical method", and the theory "cannot account for" a plateau
  linked to the free rotation. — [Maltseva et al. 2018, arXiv:1802.01497](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1802.01497)
- **Unsubstituted PAHs, same VPT2/SPECTRO approach**: main CH-stretch modes within 0.5 % of experiment (about 15 cm-1 at 3050 cm-1;
  my conversion). — [Maltseva et al., ApJ 2016, "the realm of anharmonicity"](https://bohrium.dp.tech/paper/arxiv/812400067343286272)
- **Mackie et al., PCCP 2018 (paper c7cp06546a)**: anharmonic quartic-force-field IR spectra of 9-methylanthracene,
  9,10-dimethylanthracene and four hydrogenated PAHs, the theory partner of Maltseva 2018. The full text returned 403, so neither the
  resonance treatment nor the torsion handling could be read. — [OUCI record](https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/works/4Y3WRK39);
  [RSC PDF (403 this session)](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2018/cp/c7cp06546a)
- **Sibert et al. 2014 (local-mode stretch-bend model, DFT parameters)**: CH3 bends transformed to local scissors, with a Hamiltonian
  analogous to the CH2 stretch-scissor model; jet spectra of four molecules reproduced. The abstract gives no error numbers. —
  [Sibert group page](https://sibert.chem.wisc.edu/publication/fermi-resonance-effects-in-the-vibrational-spectroscopy-of-methyl-and-methoxy-groups/)
- **Toluene, VSCF on a B3LYP surface**: "good agreement" with the Ne-matrix and liquid CH stretch spectra; matrix-site and resonance
  effects remain unresolved. No cm-1 numbers in the abstract, and no basis stated there. — [Knaanie et al. 2016, HUJI CRIS](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/infrared-spectrum-of-toluene-comparison-of-anharmonic-isolated-mo/)
- **Toluene overtones (Cavagnat et al.)**: the methyl CH stretch couples adiabatically to the quasi-free internal rotation, so the CH
  frequency depends on the torsional angle. Fermi resonances with HCH-bend combinations cause strong IVR. — [OSU abstract](https://molspect.chemistry.ohio-state.edu/symposium_53/Abstracts/p107.html)
- **VPT2+K methods reference**: Franke, Stanton, Douberly give a tutorial on VPT2+K (Fermi and Darling-Dennison polyads) for CH
  stretch spectra, called reliable for alkenes. — reported in a search-result summary; the source URL was not identified
  individually (likely [par.nsf.gov 10284833](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10284833)).

### Inferences
- Inference: in the stretch region, the Fermi-resonance effect (band splittings and intensity borrowing of tens of cm-1 between the
  symmetric CH3 stretch and the 2 x bend overtones) is larger than the torsional effect on the fundamental. The torsional effect is,
  however, the only one VPT2 cannot treat in principle when the barrier is small (V6 about 5 cm-1 in toluene). The size estimate
  comes from the band spread in the MA/DMA spectra (2870-2980 cm-1, against two harmonic bands), not from a published decomposition.
- Inference: for the **bends** the torsion problem is direct. The torsion-bend and torsion-rock cubic and quartic couplings enter the
  bends' VPT2 x-constants, and with a near-free rotor these terms are unphysical. That fits our 5.9 cm-1 shift on decoupling. For a
  V3 about 300 cm-1 rotor (4-methylimidazole, below) the torsion is a real, if anharmonic, oscillator, and keeping it in VPT2 may be
  closer to right than decoupling it. Only a reference measurement or a hindered-rotor/VCI calculation can tell.

### Gaps
- No source gave cm-1 errors of VPT2/GVPT2 (any functional) for methyl bends of methyl aromatics. Candidates to check: Barone/Bloino
  GVPT2 benchmarks on toluene or methylated heterocycles; Mackie et al. 2018 tables; the Lee/Huang/Fortenberry quartic-force-field
  work; Buragohain/Pathak methyl-PAH VPT2 (Buragohain et al., MNRAS 2020, from memory, not verified); Tabor/Sibert/Zwier on
  ethyl-, propyl- and butylbenzene (JCP 2016, from memory, not verified).
- No published head-to-head test of VPT2 with and without the torsion for a methyl aromatic was found.
- The Kjaergaard local-mode overtone work on toluene/xylenes was not reached.

## 3. Which single molecule makes the best, cheapest reference for a rotor policy? Barrier heights with sources

### Takeaway
The measured barriers split the candidates into near-free rotors (toluene and 4-picoline, V6 about 4.7-4.8 cm-1) and hindered rotors
(2-picoline V3 about 90 cm-1, the methylimidazoles 123-386 cm-1, 2-methylthiophene about 198 cm-1). Our own molecule,
4-methylimidazole, is a **hindered** rotor (V3 = 317.20 cm-1). Toluene is therefore not a like-for-like test: it is the opposite
extreme. Inference: a hindered-rotor reference with V3 in the 100-400 cm-1 range is the relevant test, but I found no molecule in
that range that has jet-cooled assignments of both its methyl stretches and its bends.

  ring pi system (supported by DFT). — Gougoula 2019 abstract, via [IDEALS](https://ideals.illinois.edu/items/113564)

### Inferences
- Inference, ranking for a rotor-policy test (≤ 15 atoms, our 3 cm-1 per family bar):
  1. **Toluene (15 atoms)** is the best-documented *free-rotor* case: gas-phase overtones with an explicit torsion-stretch model,
     Ne matrix and liquid VSCF comparison, a consistent S0 assignment for h8 and d3, and a jet S1 IR-UV study. It tests "decouple
     when V is about 0". It does not test our regime (V3 about 300 cm-1). For toluene, decoupling is almost certainly right.
  2. **2-methylthiophene (12 atoms, V3 about 198 cm-1)**, **1-methylimidazole (12 atoms, V3 185 cm-1)** and **3-methylpyrrole
     (12 atoms, V3 246 cm-1)** are in or near our barrier range and are small enough for a cheap CCSD(T)/VPT2 or VCI. I found no
     jet-cooled assigned methyl stretch/bend data for them this session. Their gas-phase FTIR band centres (NIST/PNNL) would be the
     reference, at room-temperature resolution (band contours, about ±2-5 cm-1 for band centres; this uncertainty is my estimate).
  3. **4-methylimidazole itself (12 atoms, V3 317 cm-1)** is the most direct test if a gas-phase or matrix IR spectrum of the
     monomer exists. Tautomerism (4- vs 5-methyl, which share one CH3 in different tautomers) complicates gas-phase assignment.
     That is an inference from the two tautomers having separate microwave barriers.
- Inference: since the 4/5-methylimidazole barriers (317/386 cm-1) are about 60-80x toluene's, the "near-free rotor, decouple"
  argument from toluene and the methyl PAHs (the "quasi-free rotors" of Maltseva 2018) does not carry over to our case by itself.

### Gaps
- Barrier values for 3-picoline (microwave), methylpyrazine, methylpyrimidines, 2-methylfuran (the value), 1-/2-methylnaphthalene and
  the xylenes were not obtained.
- Whether any of the hindered-rotor candidates (2-methylthiophene, 1-methylimidazole, 3-methylpyrrole, 4-methylimidazole) has
  jet-cooled or matrix-isolation IR assignments of the CH3 bends is unanswered. That is the deciding question for the choice.
- The picoline cal/mol values come from search-result abstract quotes; the primary papers (and which molecule each URL belonged
  to) still need checking before any of them is cited in a document.
