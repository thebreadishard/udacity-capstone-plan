## Guards on this file

Every row names a file or a log; no numbers without a source; stamps from `tools/stamp.py`. A row opens with its class in brackets and names
its next check (`*Next:*`); it says where things stand now and leaves the history to the ledger and git. A new open item gets its own row in
the same turn (or a line in 'Waiting on the user' when it is the user's decision); a duplicate is merged into the row it repeats. The ledger
gets the outcome, and the closed row moves verbatim to `TASKS_closed.md` (`python tools/tasks_board.py archive <reasons.json> --stamp …`) —
never struck through and left here. `python tools/tasks_board.py check` (pre-commit) refuses a struck or self-closed row, a row without class
or next check, and a number in both files, and prints the next free number.
