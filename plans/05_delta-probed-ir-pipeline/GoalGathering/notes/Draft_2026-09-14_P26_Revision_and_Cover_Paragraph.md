## 5. Added 09:2x — what the shape score's first column says about the reach product (measured this morning)

The pre-registered shape score (`PreRegistration_2026-09-14_Shape_Score_Cold_Spectra.md`) was computed for line A (PAHdb v4.00 as served) against the five jet-cooled band lists (`modules/03_lab_scoreboard/out/shape_score.md`). Intensity-weighted earth mover's distance: coronene 14.5, ovalene 16.6, peropyrene 18.2, tetracene 22.9 cm⁻¹. **The errors are not positions** (strongest-band position terms mostly under 10 cm⁻¹); they are (i) line A puts 24–42 percentage points too much intensity into the C–H out-of-plane family, and (ii) line A has no intensity at all in 1650–2000 cm⁻¹, where the laboratory lists carry 12–24 % of theirs in combination bands (Lemmens et al. 2019 said so qualitatively; this is the number per molecule). Consequence for P26 and for the goal sentence: a network that only corrects positions cannot win the shape score; the reach product must carry **intensities** (lead C: the HF→MP2 dipole change along each probed mode is free; DFT intensities are the base) and the DFT anharmonic step must deliver **combination-band intensities** (GVPT2 with intensities, pilot-note item 7). The user's decision list gains a fifth item: whether P26's output object is "per-mode position correction" (as drafted) or "position correction + intensity correction + the anharmonic combination-band step", which is what the shape criterion demands.

**Correction, 09:5x.** The family-weight check against the argon-matrix spectra (`modules/03_lab_scoreboard/out/shape_family_weights_check.md`) shows that the C–H out-of-plane over-weighting is mostly a property of the FELIX ion-dip intensities, not of DFT: matrix and line A agree on that family (tetracene 61.5 vs 68.2 %, coronene 50.3 vs 58.0 %, HBC 50.8 vs 49.5 %). What remains real by both measurements is the missing combination-band intensity above 1650 cm⁻¹ (matrix 6–13 %, line A 0 %) and the within-family shapes. Decision item 5 therefore reads: the reach product must carry the anharmonic combination-band step and intensities at least at DFT level; a coupled-cluster intensity correction (lead C) is a measured question, not yet a requirement.
