# Mandate 2026-09-13 (evening) — the affordable plan, and the ledger of obstacles on the way to it

*The user's words, 13 September, evening: "Ik wil een betaalbaar plan. Ik wil tegen de supervisor kunnen zeggen: als we dit doen op de nieuwe PC en een kleine Snellius aanvraag, dan hebben we in 2027 een neuraal netwerk getraind dat in een pipeline past waar een grote PAK ingaat en een spectrale vorm uitkomt. Blijf, ook als ik slaap, oplossingen zoeken voor de obstakels die we tegenkomen op weg naar dat doel." This note is the working document for that mandate: the goal in one sentence, the budget it must fit, the arithmetic of what the budget buys, and a ledger of obstacles with the best solution found so far, its status, and the test that decides it. It is appended, dated, every time an obstacle moves. It changes no rule of plan 05 by itself; changes it motivates are made as dated revisions and named here.*

## 1. The goal sentence (to be said to the supervisor)

> On the new desktop plus a small Snellius request, by 2027 a neural network is trained that sits in a pipeline where a large PAH goes in and a spectral shape comes out — positions, intensities and drawn widths of its infrared bands — better than the existing predictions on the reach rungs R4–R6, or with a measured error budget that is smaller than theirs (the user's success criterion, P26 memo addendum of 13 September).

Budget: the €5,000 workstation of the hardware note (16 cores, 128 GB, P13, ordered only after evidence and the supervisor's approval) and a small Snellius allocation sponsored by the supervisor. Time: the capstone calendar (defense May 2027, §12).

## 3. The pipeline the sentence describes (what exists, what is missing)

large PAH → DFT Hessian and dipole derivatives (B3LYP/6-31G*, affordable to hundreds of atoms on the desktop; PAHdb did C₃₈₄H₄₈ at 4-31G) → **network** predicts the coupled-cluster correction per mode (positions) and, if lead C delivers, the intensity correction → anharmonic shifts at DFT level as PAHdb Anharmonic does (declared, not corrected) → drawn widths under a declared broadening model → spectral shape with a per-family error budget.

Exists: DFT Hessians and modes (dry-run machinery, plan 02's Hessians, the Module 05 corpus factory), the CC label engine (frozen LNO spaces, checkpointed, resumable), the symmetry prior and probing algebra, Module 05's Transformer scaffold, the scoreboard and u_band machinery. Missing: the labels (F, g, thin decks), the intensity side (lead C), the transfer evidence (T-1/T-2), the network's training recipe on few labels (pre-train on a cheap proxy of the correction, fine-tune on CC labels — the Käser/Bowman form, §4 item 3), and the width model (declared).

## 4. Obstacle ledger (obstacle → best solution so far → status → the test that decides)

1. **Labels cost too much above naphthalene.** Thin decks (diagonal-only 2M energies; P25's free DFT ordering of the couplings; symmetry-pattern products with gradients: 18 gradients recover naphthalene's whole correction if g is small, X14) instead of full decks; tight vs xtight chosen by measurement. *Status:* F tonight; g this week (M2a, pre-registered); P25's licence after the naphthalene DFT dry run (queue item 5). *Decides:* the table in §2 recomputed with measured F and g; plan 06's decision rule (15 October).
2. **Does a correction measured on small molecules say anything about large ones?** Per-family, mode-resolved transfer tests (T-1 benzene→naphthalene, T-2 with a real CC hold-out); X17's finding that in internal coordinates the correction terminates one bond out gives a transferable *type* vocabulary (bond–adjacent bond, bond–angle sharing an atom, one apart). *Status:* T-1 waits for naphthalene's stand-in Hessian (queue item 5); **X18 (type transfer, in-plane block) is written and self-tested on benzene (21:5x), runs the moment item 5 delivers**; sub-obstacle 2a: the out-of-plane block needs a symmetry-covariant sign convention for wags and torsions before its types can transfer (idea: orient every wag to +z and define the torsion's sign by the twist of its two outer atoms, then test on benzene→benzene). *Decides:* RMS ≤ 2.5 cm⁻¹ per judged family (agreed threshold); losing condition in P26 §6.
3. **What the network must learn may be too large for few labels.** Shrink it first with a better functional (lead A: a double hybrid as the DFT half if it removes ≥ 2/3 of the correction; reading thresholds confirmed), then pre-train the network on a cheap proxy of the correction computable for thousands of PAHs on the desktop (the BHHLYP−B3LYP stand-in the Module 05 corpus already plans, or a double-hybrid−B3LYP proxy if lead A shows it is closer to CC) and fine-tune on the CC labels of §2 — the transfer-learning form of Käser et al. 2021 and the Δ-ML form of Qu et al. 2021, applied to Hessian corrections instead of surfaces — this pre-train/fine-tune recipe is already written into Module 05's RECIPE (dated P26 amendment of 13 September: the ωB97X − B3LYP corpus on Hessian QM9 subsets for pre-training, fine-tuning on pipeline B's thin-deck labels); what is new here is only the choice of proxy, decided by lead A's test. *Status:* `probes/dh_diagonal_baseline.py` ready (queue item 5a); the proxy-validity test is the same run (CC vs DH−B3LYP vs BHHLYP−B3LYP per mode at R0 and R1). *Decides:* r ≤ 1/3 (collapse into "double hybrid as DFT half") / ≥ 2/3 (fit or learn the whole correction).
4. **No laboratory truth at large sizes; "better than what exists" must still be grounded.** Hold-out molecules with measured corrections (the thin decks of §2 are the hold-outs), the pipeline's own error budget per family, and one cold resolved measurement where it can be had (lead G, in the letter, §13 item 3). *Status:* criterion agreed; G asked. *Decides:* the supervisor's answer on G; T-2's numbers.
5. **Shape is more than positions: intensities and widths.** Intensities: lead C (dipoles at every probed geometry are free at SCF/MP2 level; HF→MP2 intensity change along each mode tells whether correlation moves intensities where DFT fails, the 6.2/7.7 µm ratio) and DFT intensities as the base; widths: a declared broadening model (absorption at 0 K plus a stated width; astronomical emission profiles are the domain of cascade models and are not promised). *Status (14 Sep):* C is one flag in the probe scripts (the scripts are no longer live; to add before the next deck); the width model is declared in the pilot-note skeleton §C (14 Sep: a fixed 10 cm⁻¹ Lorentzian for figures only; no score depends on it); **measured 14 Sep on line A: the shape score's largest terms are family-weight errors (C–H out-of-plane over-weighted by 24–42 pp) and the missing combination-band intensity (12–24 % of the laboratory intensity in 1650–2000 cm⁻¹), not positions** — intensities and anharmonic combination bands are the first obstacle on the shape objective. *Decides:* whether the HF→MP2 intensity table on benzene/naphthalene shows a correction large enough to learn.
6. **Compute that exists only on paper.** The desktop is a priced configuration, not a purchase (gated on evidence and approval); Snellius is a request the supervisor must sponsor; the laptop is at its memory ceiling at naphthalene. *Status:* F tonight is the evidence for the request's size; the request's content under P26 is naphthalene full plus thin decks, not full decks at pyrene. *Decides:* the user's purchase decision; the supervisor's sponsorship.

## 5a. Obstacles and ideas added by the standing search (dated; ideas are ideas until a test says otherwise)

11. **The labels themselves carry a basis-set error larger than the method error.** (13 Sep, evening.) The anchor is LNO-CCSD(T)/cc-pVTZ; M1 §2.2d measured the canonical curvature's basis dependence at +134/−67/−145 cm⁻¹ per benzene mode from DZ to TZ, two thirds SCF, and decision 33 carries a 3–11 cm⁻¹ basis term against a local-correlation bias of 0.1–0.2 cm⁻¹. A network trained on TZ labels learns TZ truth. *Best solution:* the composite anchor P18 (SCF and MP2 at QZ/5Z are cheap — the 27-point line took nineteen minutes — plus the CC correction at TZ); Semidalas & Martin 2023 (composite methods for harmonic frequencies, abstract) is the literature to read. *Status (corrected 14 Sep, 11:0x — the ledger was behind the README):* P18 was **adopted on 12 September as decision 33**: the anchor energy per point is LNO-CCSD(T)(xtight, TZ) + [MP2(full) − MP2(LNO)] + [MP2/QZ − MP2/TZ] + [SCF/5Z − SCF/TZ], every term a computed difference, under 1 % of an LNO point; the cheap basis line of the same day (`probes/results_m1/BASIS_LINE_scf_mp2.md`) measured the TZ → QZ/5Z terms on benzene's three probed modes: SCF +1.8/+3.8, −2.4/−2.8, −4.6/−3.1 cm⁻¹ (QZ/5Z), MP2 correlation −1.0, −2.4, −8.0 cm⁻¹ (QZ) — 1–8 cm⁻¹ against a frozen-space bias of 0.1–0.2, which is why the composite is the anchor. What remains open is decision 26's term: the basis change of the CCSD(T) − MP2 remainder beyond TZ, unmeasured (a QZ CC point at benzene would measure it; not affordable at naphthalene). The 14 September budget documents already price the composite's terms (60 s MP2/QZ + 353 s SCF/5Z per naphthalene point). *Decides (remaining):* one benzene CC/QZ point per probed mode, if ever affordable; otherwise the term stays a declared bound.
12. **The DFT side of the pipeline at R6 has never been timed by this project.** (13 Sep, evening.) A B3LYP/6-31G* Hessian of C₃₈₄H₄₈ (432 atoms) is the network's *input*; PAHdb computed such Hessians at 4-31G, this project has timed nothing above pyrene. *Best solution:* time a coronene B3LYP Hessian on the laptop after the run (plan 02 has Hessians to pyrene), extrapolate with the measured scaling, and if the desktop cannot do R6-size Hessians in days, the pipeline's input at R6 is PAHdb's own Hessian data or a fragment scheme. *Status (14 Sep, 08:1x — a measurement the project already had):* plan 02 timed on this laptop, psi4 1.11, B3LYP/6-31G* frequency jobs including optimisation: benzene 4.2 min, pyrene 54 min, coronene **174 min** (Compute Budget 2026-09-14 §3.1). Coronene (36 atoms, 24 C) to C₃₈₄H₄₈ (432 atoms) is a factor 12 in atoms; analytic Hessians scale between N³ and N⁴ in this regime, so one R6-size Hessian is 174 min × 12^3–12^4 ≈ 200–3,000 days on the laptop, 60–1,300 days on the desktop by the core-count estimate. **The DFT side at R6 is therefore not a desktop computation either**; the pipeline's input for R6-class molecules is PAHdb's own Hessian-level data where it exists (line A computed C₃₈₄H₄₈ at 4-31G), a fragment scheme, or a neural-network force field (obstacle 14's lever, licence permitting). Layer D of idea I5 is bounded by the same arithmetic: coronene-size pairs are hours, circumcoronene-size (54 C) ≈ 174 min × (66/36)^3.5 ≈ 24 h each on the laptop — a few hundred of them is desktop-months, which fixes layer D's reach at roughly 50–60 carbons. *Decides:* one re-timed coronene Hessian with the plan-05 factory (queue item 3, minutes to hours) to replace the plan-02 number; the exponent from benzene–pyrene–coronene on the same machine.
