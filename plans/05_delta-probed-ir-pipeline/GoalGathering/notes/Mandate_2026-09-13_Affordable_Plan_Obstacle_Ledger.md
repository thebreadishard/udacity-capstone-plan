# Mandate 2026-09-13 (evening) — the affordable plan, and the ledger of obstacles on the way to it

*The user's words, 13 September, evening: "Ik wil een betaalbaar plan. Ik wil tegen de supervisor kunnen zeggen: als we dit doen op de nieuwe PC en een kleine Snellius aanvraag, dan hebben we in 2027 een neuraal netwerk getraind dat in een pipeline past waar een grote PAK ingaat en een spectrale vorm uitkomt. Blijf, ook als ik slaap, oplossingen zoeken voor de obstakels die we tegenkomen op weg naar dat doel." This note is the working document for that mandate: the goal in one sentence, the budget it must fit, the arithmetic of what the budget buys, and a ledger of obstacles with the best solution found so far, its status, and the test that decides it. It is appended, dated, every time an obstacle moves. It changes no rule of plan 05 by itself; changes it motivates are made as dated revisions and named here.*

## 1. The goal sentence (to be said to the supervisor)

> On the new desktop plus a small Snellius request, by 2027 a neural network is trained that sits in a pipeline where a large PAH goes in and a spectral shape comes out — positions, intensities and drawn widths of its infrared bands — better than the existing predictions on the reach rungs R4–R6, or with a measured error budget that is smaller than theirs (the user's success criterion, P26 memo addendum of 13 September).

Budget: the €5,000 workstation of the hardware note (16 cores, 128 GB, P13, ordered only after evidence and the supervisor's approval) and a small Snellius allocation sponsored by the supervisor. Time: the capstone calendar (defense May 2027, §12).

## 2. What the budget buys, in labels per year (duration table, P26 block; F provisional 2.27, desktop 2.3–3.8× the laptop, plus a small Snellius share)

| label class | full deck | diagonal-only deck | gradients (if g ≤ 5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| benzene-class (M ≈ 30) | 80–160 molecules/yr | 290–570 | 900–2,900 |

Reading: the truth the network can be trained on in one year is **one or two full naphthalene-class corrections, about ten diagonal-only naphthalene-class corrections, a hundred benzene-class corrections, one or two thin pyrene-class checks, and at most one coronene-class check** — more if g is small (gradients) and P25 is licensed. Everything above pyrene size that the network says is *reach*, not truth, and is judged on hold-outs and a grounded error budget. This is the honest content of the goal sentence; the arithmetic is remade the moment F (tonight) and g (this week) are measured.

## 3. The pipeline the sentence describes (what exists, what is missing)

large PAH → DFT Hessian and dipole derivatives (B3LYP/6-31G*, affordable to hundreds of atoms on the desktop; PAHdb did C₃₈₄H₄₈ at 4-31G) → **network** predicts the coupled-cluster correction per mode (positions) and, if lead C delivers, the intensity correction → anharmonic shifts at DFT level as PAHdb Anharmonic does (declared, not corrected) → drawn widths under a declared broadening model → spectral shape with a per-family error budget.

Exists: DFT Hessians and modes (dry-run machinery, plan 02's Hessians, the Module 05 corpus factory), the CC label engine (frozen LNO spaces, checkpointed, resumable), the symmetry prior and probing algebra, Module 05's Transformer scaffold, the scoreboard and u_band machinery. Missing: the labels (F, g, thin decks), the intensity side (lead C), the transfer evidence (T-1/T-2), the network's training recipe on few labels (pre-train on a cheap proxy of the correction, fine-tune on CC labels — the Käser/Bowman form, §4 item 3), and the width model (declared).

## 4. Obstacle ledger (obstacle → best solution so far → status → the test that decides)

1. **Labels cost too much above naphthalene.** Thin decks (diagonal-only 2M energies; P25's free DFT ordering of the couplings; symmetry-pattern products with gradients: 18 gradients recover naphthalene's whole correction if g is small, X14) instead of full decks; tight vs xtight chosen by measurement. *Status:* F tonight; g this week (M2a, pre-registered); P25's licence after the naphthalene DFT dry run (queue item 5). *Decides:* the table in §2 recomputed with measured F and g; plan 06's decision rule (15 October).
2. **Does a correction measured on small molecules say anything about large ones?** Per-family, mode-resolved transfer tests (T-1 benzene→naphthalene, T-2 with a real CC hold-out); X17's finding that in internal coordinates the correction terminates one bond out gives a transferable *type* vocabulary (bond–adjacent bond, bond–angle sharing an atom, one apart). *Status:* T-1 waits for naphthalene's stand-in Hessian (queue item 5); X17 type transfer benzene→naphthalene is the next desk test after it. *Decides:* RMS ≤ 2.5 cm⁻¹ per judged family (agreed threshold); losing condition in P26 §6.
3. **What the network must learn may be too large for few labels.** Shrink it first with a better functional (lead A: a double hybrid as the DFT half if it removes ≥ 2/3 of the correction; reading thresholds confirmed), then pre-train the network on a cheap proxy of the correction computable for thousands of PAHs on the desktop (the BHHLYP−B3LYP stand-in the Module 05 corpus already plans, or a double-hybrid−B3LYP proxy if lead A shows it is closer to CC) and fine-tune on the CC labels of §2 — the transfer-learning form of Käser et al. 2021 and the Δ-ML form of Qu et al. 2021, applied to Hessian corrections instead of surfaces — this pre-train/fine-tune recipe is already written into Module 05's RECIPE (dated P26 amendment of 13 September: the ωB97X − B3LYP corpus on Hessian QM9 subsets for pre-training, fine-tuning on pipeline B's thin-deck labels); what is new here is only the choice of proxy, decided by lead A's test. *Status:* `probes/dh_diagonal_baseline.py` ready (queue item 5a); the proxy-validity test is the same run (CC vs DH−B3LYP vs BHHLYP−B3LYP per mode at R0 and R1). *Decides:* r ≤ 1/3 (collapse into "double hybrid as DFT half") / ≥ 2/3 (fit or learn the whole correction).
4. **No laboratory truth at large sizes; "better than what exists" must still be grounded.** Hold-out molecules with measured corrections (the thin decks of §2 are the hold-outs), the pipeline's own error budget per family, and one cold resolved measurement where it can be had (lead G, in the letter, §13 item 3). *Status:* criterion agreed; G asked. *Decides:* the supervisor's answer on G; T-2's numbers.
5. **Shape is more than positions: intensities and widths.** Intensities: lead C (dipoles at every probed geometry are free at SCF/MP2 level; HF→MP2 intensity change along each mode tells whether correlation moves intensities where DFT fails, the 6.2/7.7 µm ratio) and DFT intensities as the base; widths: a declared broadening model (absorption at 0 K plus a stated width; astronomical emission profiles are the domain of cascade models and are not promised). *Status:* C is one flag in the probe scripts, added after the current run (the scripts are live); the width model is a paragraph to write. *Decides:* whether the HF→MP2 intensity table on benzene/naphthalene shows a correction large enough to learn.
6. **Compute that exists only on paper.** The desktop is a priced configuration, not a purchase (gated on evidence and approval); Snellius is a request the supervisor must sponsor; the laptop is at its memory ceiling at naphthalene. *Status:* F tonight is the evidence for the request's size; the request's content under P26 is naphthalene full plus thin decks, not full decks at pyrene. *Decides:* the user's purchase decision; the supervisor's sponsorship.
7. **Gradients are the biggest lever and no one has printed their price.** M2a measures g in PySCFAD with the shipped engine; M2 (frozen-space gradients, 2–3 weeks) only if g ≤ 20. *Status:* installed, pre-registered, runs after the current run. *Decides:* g ≤ 6 / ≤ 20 / > 20.
8. **The calendar.** Proposal in September; R1 full deck on Snellius November–December (17–28 Snellius-days at 4 nodes, or the desktop in months); thin decks and benzene-class labels January–March; network training March–April; defense May 2027. *Status:* §12 is still the pre-P26 calendar and is revised after F with the table the user asked for. *Decides:* F and the supervisor's reading time.
9. **Cations.** Astronomically as important as neutrals; the proxy corpus can carry them at DFT level, CC labels need unrestricted local CC (pyscf-forge has `ULNOCCSD_T`, per the API notes of 12 September). *Status:* phased in P26; nothing built. *Decides:* after neutrals transfer.
10. **Anharmonicity stays at DFT level.** Positions = corrected harmonic + DFT anharmonic shift (as PAHdb Anharmonic); declared in §6, the Δ₂ = 0 null row separates the two. *Status:* declared; no obstacle to the goal sentence, a limit on its claim.
