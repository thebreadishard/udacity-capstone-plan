# Design note, 8 October 2026, 18:0x — what an anchor of the size ladder's top rung costs, per route (TASKS 34, step 1)

*The user, 8 Oct 17:4x: anchors stop at the top rung of the size ladder; the network carries the correction to the mandate's large PAHs; affordable
CC couplings are needed only up to that rung. This note prices the rung from our own logs before any run. No new measurement.*

| anchor | atoms | point group | gradients | machine, lanes × threads | median per gradient | wall time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| benzonitrile | 13 | C2v | 54 | CPX62, 3 × 8/16 | 8 min | 5.8 h |
| naphthalene | 18 | D2h | 30 | CCX53, 3 × 8/16 | 28 min | 19.1 h |
| anthracene | 24 | D2h | 42 | CCX53, 5 × 1/8/16 | 362 min | 117 h |

From naphthalene to anthracene the time per gradient grows 13× for 1.33× the atoms — an exponent near 9, steeper than the textbook N⁷ for
CCSD(T), partly because anthracene ran on more lanes with fewer threads each. Below, both exponents (7 and 9) bracket the estimate. LNO-CCSD(T)
energies: 6,823 s per energy for naphthalene at xtight thresholds and 8 threads (8 Oct); ≥ 9 h for phenanthrene+CN (25 atoms) at tight (L2, 25 Sep).

## 3. The routes for couplings above anthracene

- **(a) LNO energies for the couplings: out.** A full Hessian from energies needs of the order of (6N)²/2 points (≈ 18,000 for perylene before
  symmetry, a few thousand after), at 2–10 h per LNO energy.
- **(b) Canonical CC up to pyrene: affordable but slow.** Pyrene at 4–5 server days is above the ≤ 3-day line of TASKS 34, but it is a single
  anchor per family; perylene is not affordable this way.
- **(c) Partial decks (decision 58): not today.** The 4 Oct rehearsal showed that network-predicted rows cannot replace measured ones yet.
- **(d) An LNO gradient: development, not a purchase.** pyscf-forge has none; writing our own is allowed (the user, 13 Sep) but is a project of
  its own.
- **(e) Spot-check anchors (new): LNO diagonal curvatures as a test, not as training data.** At a few chosen coordinates of a 26–40-atom
  molecule, LNO-CCSD(T) at xtight gives H_kk within 0.27 % of canonical (8 Oct, three coordinates of naphthalene), two energies per coordinate.
  Five coordinates of a 30-atom molecule cost of the order of 10 energies × 2–10 h — days of laptop or server time, not weeks. This tests the
  network's predicted diagonal above the anchors' size; couplings above the top rung stay untested and are carried by the network.

## 4. Proposal (for the user)

The top rung of full anchors is **anthracene to pyrene (24–26 atoms)**, canonical CCSD(T) with the MP2 basis step. Above it, **spot-check
anchors (e)** on two or three molecules of 30–40 atoms (perylene, benzo[a]pyrene from pool 3), so that the size extrapolation is tested on the
diagonal at CC level, beside TASKS 35's test at the proxy level. Couplings above 26 atoms are a claim of the network, stated as such. Route (d)
stays on the long-term list; it would be the lever that turns the spot checks into full anchors. Nothing runs before the user's word and a
registration.

## 5. Spot-check anchors — draft of the registration (the user, 8 Oct 20:1x: "Akkoord met de steekproef-ankers"; TASKS 36)

*Draft, 8 Oct 20:5x. It becomes the registration when the two geometries exist and the coordinates are drawn — before the first LNO energy.*

- **Molecules.** Perylene (32 atoms) and benzo[a]pyrene (32 atoms), from pool 3's neutral rows (their B3LYP geometries and FD Hessians).
- **Coordinates, by rule.** Per molecule: the symmetry-unique atoms sorted by index; the first C and the first H of that list; for each of the two,
  the out-of-plane direction and one in-plane direction (the plane from the smallest principal axis, as in `lno_curvature_check.py`) — four
  Cartesian coordinates per molecule, chosen before any prediction is computed. Benzo[a]pyrene has no symmetry: its first C and first H by index.
- **The check value.** H_kk(spot) = H_kk(LNO-CCSD(T)/cc-pVDZ, xtight, `--spot`) + [H_kk(MP2/cc-pVTZ) − H_kk(MP2/cc-pVDZ)]
  (`cc_composite_full_check.py compute --ks`), the TZ tier of the anchors. Its own uncertainty: LNO's +0.27 % (8 Oct), stated beside every number.
- **The prediction.** H_low(B3LYP, analytic where it exists) + ΔH of chain 34's models head-tuned on all six TZ-tier anchors (λ = 1, the standard of
  amendment (A)), seed mean of three. Needs a T3 mode that fits on all anchors and predicts a molecule that is not an anchor (to be built and tested
  first; today T3 only leaves one anchor out).
- **Read.** Per coordinate and pooled: |H_kk(pred) − H_kk(spot)| against the zero rule |H_kk(B3LYP) − H_kk(spot)|. **Lines (draft):** ratio of
  the pooled errors ≤ 0.5 → the network carries the CC correction of the diagonal beyond the anchors' size; ≥ 1.0 → it does not (no better than
  the low level there); between → noted. A coordinate whose network error is within the LNO error bar counts as a pass for that coordinate.
- **Cost.** 8 coordinates × 2 LNO energies at xtight (≈ 2–10 h each for 32 atoms; perylene's is measured on its reference point first) plus the MP2
  rows; days on the laptop or the CCX53.
