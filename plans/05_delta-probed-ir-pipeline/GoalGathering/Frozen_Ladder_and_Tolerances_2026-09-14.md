## 1. Three sentence types, declared up front

- **Accuracy rungs (A).** Laboratory data exist. The claim depends on the rung (decision 28):
  - *R0 is an agreement rung.* The claim is **agree with each scored laboratory band within the laboratory band uncertainty combined with the pipeline's own error budget** (noise, freezing bias, basis-set line, locality) — for positions and, under decision 18, for the integrated intensities against the source's stated uncertainty. Passing licenses the anchor and the recovery; failing means the budget is incomplete and nothing moves up the ladder. The paired comparison with the opponents is printed at R0 but claims nothing; the word "beat" is not written for benzene.
  - *R1 does both:* agreement is required (the local-CC licence rung) and, per family, the question whether the coupled-cluster correction adds accuracy over DFT is answered on the room-temperature naphthalene sources named in §2 (decision 21).
  - *R2–R3:* "beat the frozen line per band against the laboratory scoreboard" (`Frozen_Lines_to_Beat.md` §5), per family, wherever the laboratory can decide it under the decidability rule of §2.
- **Reach rungs (R).** No per-molecule laboratory spectrum exists. The claim is *the pipeline ran end-to-end and produced a spectrum with a stated error budget*; comparisons against the lines are **theory-vs-theory and labelled as such**. The word "beat" is forbidden on reach rungs.
- **Cost sentences.** Exactly two kinds exist, and this is the only place that defines them; every other document conforms.
  - **The cost record** — allowed on any rung once printed, and promised for every rung that ran, one per mode that ran on the rung: `K = n energies|gradients (mode E: of which 2M = … in the single-mode ± block, K_off = … energies (… ± pairs of off-diagonal patterns); K_off at the common threshold ρ*_common = …, NOT_RUN until the Q8(c) probe re-prints the record with both neighbours' values) at rung R, mode E|G, prior = structural|learned, σ = …, c₀ = …, q₂ block = 2·M_scored energies (outside K), RMS_resp = …, ρ_noise = …, ρ_dry = …, c = …, ρ* = …, ρ(K) = …, PNO extrapolation = none|CPS, wall-clock w per probe on machine m, printed by probes/<file>`. Nothing else about cost may be written.
  - **The size sentence** — numeric only, in one of two forms, each allowed only after Q8(c) has passed for that quantity at R1→R2 and R2→R3 with the structural prior, both counts read from the rungs' stored ρ(n) curves at the **common threshold** ρ\*_common = max(ρ\*(R_n), ρ\*(R_{n+1})) (ρ\* is rung-dependent, so the record K_off alone would report threshold drift as a property of Δ₂). *Mode-E form* (always earnable, because mode E runs on every rung R1–R3 that runs): "K_off went n₁ → n₂ → n₃ from R1 to R3 while the mode count went M₁ → M₂ → M₃". *Mode-G form* (only if mode G was licensed and ran on R1, R2 and R3 — side-project milestones M3, M4, M5 with both checks each): the same for K. The adjectives "size-independent", "O(1)", "saturates", "does not grow", with or without "-class", are forbidden everywhere, including the Module 08 paper. The planning expectation behind the sentence (decision 13, from the benzene dry run's measured K_off = 388 of 435) is K_off ≈ M(M−1)/2 in mode E where no prior bites, so any favourable size sentence is expected to come from the symmetry prior (decision 11) or the Module 05 prior and is worded against the rung's free-element count.
  - **"Beat" and noise.** A rung carries "beat" language only if the Q6 noise line **of the mode that produced its scored Δ₂** (σ_E line for mode E, σ_g line for mode G; §3) passed at that rung's size class; otherwise it carries a cost record and no "beat".

**R6 in one sentence (decision 1).** R6 is promised as fragment-probed Δ₂, conditional on the fragment licence of §3 and on B3; families that fail the licence are withdrawn from the R6 certificate with their measured long-range share; if all scored families fail, R6 is reported with the refusal sentence.

**Decidability per family.** A family is scored against gas-phase data wherever gas data exist for that molecule and family. It is **decidable** if the **measured band-centre uncertainty** u_band of the gas scoreboard for that family is smaller than the family's beat margin, where u_band is the quadrature sum of:
- (i) the **instrument resolution** as stated by the source's documentation — never the JCAMP point spacing;
- (ii) the **centroid precision** from the spectrum's signal-to-noise;
- (iii) a **temperature term** u_T, by source class:
  - *hot-vapour source:* u_T = χ_F·(T_source − 296 K) + u_296, with χ_F the family's measured temperature slope from the heat-pipe study of item 52 (Table 1: 6.2 µm 0.038–0.044, 7.7 µm 0.022–0.024, 8.5–8.8 µm 0.008–0.010, 11.8–12 µm 0.014–0.023 cm⁻¹ K⁻¹; a family without a measured slope takes the largest measured 6–15 µm slope, 0.044, as its floor; a molecule without its own slopes takes pyrene's as a labelled stand-in), the paper's ±0.5 K thermocouple, ±30 % of the correction, and u_296 the 0 → 296 K shift per family computed by Module 03 from item 52's Bose-population model with the molecule's mean bath frequency (the PAHdb theoretical modes below 700 cm⁻¹; 1–6 cm⁻¹ across the measured families); T_source is the source's stated temperature, or the written assumption where none is stated (245 °C Coblentz; 250 °C GC-IRD lightpipe, an assumption labelled as such);
  - *room-temperature source with unresolved bands (the NIST Quantitative IR benzene record; the PNNL naphthalene record):* u_T is the pipeline's own computed 0 → T_source shift per scored band, δ_i(T) = Σ_k X_ik n̄_k(T), from the DFT anharmonic constants of Module 03's step, applied as a correction to the 0 K prediction and carrying ±30 % of itself plus the source's temperature uncertainty (decision 29); until the X_ik print, Module 03 prints the floor form above and labels it;
  - *room-temperature source with resolved fundamentals (Pirali 2009):* u_T = 0 — the 0 → 1 transition read directly does not shift with temperature; the hot bands are resolved away — plus a head-to-origin term of 0.5 cm⁻¹, an upper bound read from the Q-branch widths and labelled as such until it is measured on a digitised spectrum (decision 21);
  - *rotationally resolved band origin (Albert 2011, Pirali 2013):* u_T = 0 and no head-to-origin term; the fit uncertainty of the origin enters when read from the paper; until then u_band is the reading precision, labelled a lower bound.

Module 03 prints, per spectrum, the source class (cell / vapour cell / GC-IRD / jet / band origin), the stated temperature and the stated resolution as columns, and u_band and the verdict per molecule and family **before the pilot note**; the pilot note's item 1 records per family *gas-decidable / matrix-gated / inconclusive by construction*. A family with matrix data only passes through the **matrix–gas gate**: if the Module 03 measured |matrix − gas| offset for that family is not smaller than its beat margin, it is scored "pre-declared inconclusive on matrix" — not "beat", not "lost" (measured so far, 10 K matrix against hot vapour: median offsets +3.3 to +5.9 cm⁻¹ in the six families with n ≥ 6, a zero offset rejected; the equal-temperature matrix shift and the gate's margins are the pilot note's). R0–R1 are gas-scored throughout; no matrix gate applies there. The PAHdb matrix intensities are scaled to a computed sum, so the matrix column scores relative intensities only; no absolute matrix intensity is ever scored.

## 3. Fixed now (not pilot-dependent)

- **Reporting unit:** cm⁻¹ per band; families = C–H stretch (~3.3 µm), C–C modes (6.2 / 7.7 / 8.6 µm), C–H out-of-plane by adjacency class (solo / duo / trio / quartet, 10–15 µm).
- **Resolution floor:** no claim finer than **10 cm⁻¹** in any astronomical framing; a lab-facing claim may be finer only if u_band *and* the declared controls (ρ, the local-CC noise floor, the threshold sensitivity) support it, printed by the comparison probe — never finer than the scoreboard's own uncertainty.
- **Matrix tolerance:** working convention **15 cm⁻¹**; the binding value is the Module 03 measured one, fixed in the pilot note (§4 item 4). Gas phase is preferred over matrix wherever both exist and u_band allows.
- **Comparison form (pre-registered):** paired per-band absolute error, pipeline versus line, on identical laboratory bands; per family; mean ± spread; at least three seeds for every ML component. **Inconclusive is a publishable outcome.** The scoreboard is never a training, validation or pattern-design input of the pipeline (the Module 04 baseline is the declared exception). **No laboratory band may be scored twice under different windows.**
- **Anchor basis fixed per rung in the deck, and the same basis on both arms of every comparison:** cc-pVTZ for R0 and R1 (the licence rungs); the R2–R6 basis is a deck number fixed before that rung's first probe. The Q6 bias line compares Δ₂(frozen) and Δ₂(canonical) in the same basis. The canonical feasibility probe has printed (Budget §3.1): the Q6 bias line (61 = 1 + 2·30 energies, the diagonal along benzene's 30 modes) fits the B2 laptop; the full canonical reference Hessian (72 canonical gradients, or 1,801 energies by central mixed differences in the modes) does not — by memory for the gradients and by time for the energies. Consequently, Q7(i) at R0 compares to the local-CC reference only, Q7(iv) reads the reference Hessian from the local-CC arm, and the full canonical Hessian is the first B3 request; that sentence is printed with the R0 result. A bias line measured in a smaller basis would be a lower bound on the cc-pVTZ freezing bias; the R0 bias line is measured at cc-pVTZ.
- **The promised correction is Δ₂ only.** No CC correction to cubic or quartic constants is promised; the diagonal-cubic probe is a bonus number. DFT anharmonic constants are computed for a family set closed under the resonance search **to closure depth one** (a scored family mode's partners; the partners' own diagonal anharmonicity from their 1-D cut only), bounded by the polyad cap; the pilot note prints the closed set's size and Hessian count per rung.
- **K is a measurement, not a choice — with a noise-aware stopping rule.** Every pattern p enters the Q0 deck as the pair ±p, and the mode-E response is the symmetric combination R_s(p) = ½[ΔE(+p) + ΔE(−p)] − ΔE(0) = ½ pᵀΔ₂p + O(p⁴): the first-order term Δ₁·p — the CC−DFT force at the DFT geometry, not zero — and the cubic term cancel exactly. The antisymmetric combination R_a(p) = ½[ΔE(+p) − ΔE(−p)] = Δ₁·p + O(p³) gives Δ₁ from the single-mode block and φ_iii from the second amplitude. **Δ₁ is load-bearing:** the recovered Δ₂ is the Hessian correction at the DFT minimum, and the corrected surface's own minimum lies at δq_j = −Δ₁,j/(ω_j + Δ₂,jj) along the totally symmetric modes; the harmonic constants there differ by Σ_j φ_iij δq_j. **Rule:** the scored harmonic part is Δ₂ + Σ_j φ_iij^DFT δq_j — the first-order corrected-surface-minimum term, with φ_iij from the DFT cubic set (which therefore includes the totally symmetric modes) and δq_j from Δ₁ — printed per band in the error budget with Δ₁ per totally symmetric mode; no atom is moved and no geometry is re-optimised; the Δ = 0 null arm and the Q7 comparison are unaffected. The mode-G response ∇ΔE(p) − ∇ΔE(0) removes Δ₁ by construction. **K counts energies in mode E (a ± pair counts 2) and gradients in mode G**; ΔE(0) is one shared reference energy per rung, computed once; its error c₀ is a common offset to every R_s. At a single pattern amplitude that offset is collinear with a uniform shift of every diagonal element, so it is **identified, not fitted:** on every scored family's mode the single-mode block carries R_s at two amplitudes, q_s and q₂, so Δ₂,ii = 2[R_s(q₂) − R_s(q_s)]/(q₂² − q_s²) is c₀-free and c₀ = R_s(q_s) − ½Δ₂,ii q_s² is over-determined across those modes; its mean is subtracted from every response before the recovery and printed in the cost record beside σ. The q₂ energies (two per scored mode) are outside K: K = 2M + K_off, with the q₂ block its own line in the cost record. The same two-amplitude read removes the quartic contamination on the scored modes exactly; on the multi-atom patterns that term is a labelled bias printed after the note from the sealed degree-4 fits. The per-response scatter is σ(R_s) = σ_E/√2. Patterns are consumed in the hashed order of the Q0 deck, pairs together, and ρ(n) is evaluated after each complete pair, n counted in energies. **In mode E the residual is the off-diagonal one** (decision 12): after the first block (the 2M single-mode pairs, which fix the diagonal), each held-out response is reduced to R_s,off = R_s − R_s^diag, and ρ_off(n) = RMS over held-out patterns of (predicted − computed R_s,off) ÷ RMS of the computed R_s,off, with ρ_noise,off = σ(R_s)/RMS_resp,off (on benzene the off-diagonal part is 2.4 % of the response, so a rule on raw ρ would stop at K_off = 2 with 7 cm⁻¹ diagonal-only errors in place; on ρ_off the same deck needed K_off = 388). Mode G has no diagonal block and reads ρ. σ_E, σ_g are the pooled per-arm scatters of the §3 estimator from the largest Q6 noise measurement at or below the rung's size that exists before the rung's first probe (which one, printed in the cost record). **K is the smallest n at which ρ(n) ≤ ρ\*, with ρ\* = max(1.1·ρ_dry, c·ρ_noise)**: c ≥ 1 the pilot-note constant of §4 item 8, and ρ_dry(rung, mode) the **model floor** — the residual the quadratic model reaches on the noiseless dry run of that rung and mode with all training pairs (the quartic and higher contamination the Hessian model cannot fit at q_s), printed by the dry run beside K_cap, never typed (benzene: ≈ 0.2 on ρ_off). Equivalently, the held-out χ² per response with σ(R_s) (mode E) or σ_g (mode G) as the per-response sigma first falls to c². **Two guards close the rule.** (i) *Floor:* the trivial recovery Δ₂ ≡ 0 has ρ = 1 at every n, so the rule is evaluated only when ρ\* < ρ_max = 0.5 (a recovery at ρ = 0.5 explains three quarters of the held-out variance); if ρ\* ≥ 0.5 the rung's responses in that mode are "at noise", K reads NOT_RUN(at noise), no Δ₂ is recovered in that mode, and the refusal sentence is written. (ii) *Minimum count:* in mode E the deck's first block is the 2M single-mode patterns (±q_s along each DFT mode), consumed before any multi-atom pattern, and the rule is evaluated only for n > 2M, so K_off ≥ 2; in mode G there is no diagonal block and the rule is evaluated only for n ≥ n_min(G), a count fixed in §4 item 9. K is never written down before the rung runs. The pilot note fixes a cap K_cap per rung and per mode (§4 item 9) from the **noise-injected** dry run — never from the noiseless one. If ρ has not reached ρ\* by K_cap, the rung's Δ₂ is "not recovered at cap" (§5 item 4), and the cap is never raised to rescue it. The cost record carries σ, RMS_resp, ρ_noise, ρ_dry, c and ρ(K) beside K, so a small K on a rung with small responses reads as what it is. The dry run's printed ρ_off column (`probes/dryrun_dft_delta_recovery.py`, stage C) is the reference implementation.
- **Hold-out membership is decided before any response exists:** by a seeded rule in the Q0 deck (deck seed + pair index; one deck index per pair ±p, and the pair is the hold-out unit — a pair is never split between hold-out and training), fraction f_h (§4 item 10).
- **Frozen spaces — the object, written once.** At the reference geometry the local-CC code stores the localized occupied orbitals and each fragment's LNO vectors in the AO basis. At a displaced geometry **both halves are transported by projection, and nothing is re-localised or assigned**: the occupied set is C_occ(x) = Löwdin[P_occ(x) C_occ(0)], P_occ(x) the projector onto the displaced geometry's occupied space (no localiser runs at a displaced geometry, so no assignment exists and no permutation can switch; π localisation on the D₆h rungs is soft, and re-localise-and-assign would mix); the stored virtual-space vectors are likewise projected onto the new geometry's virtual space and Löwdin-orthonormalised; that projected, orthonormalised pair of sets is "the frozen space", and the correlation energy is evaluated in it, **with the transported active blocks semicanonicalised at the displaced geometry** (a rotation inside the frozen space that leaves the space unchanged; the impurity solvers assume diagonal orbital energies for their MP2 start and their (T); decision 14). **The energy per point is the composite** (decision 15): E_LNO-CCSD(T) + [E_MP2(full) − E_MP2(LNO)], the standard correction for the truncated space, with MP2(full) canonical and roughness-free; **plus two basis terms** (decision 33): E_anchor = E_LNO-CCSD(T)(xtight, TZ) + [E_MP2(full, TZ) − E_MP2(LNO, TZ)] + [E_MP2(full, QZ) − E_MP2(full, TZ)] + [E_SCF(5Z) − E_SCF(TZ)], every term a difference of computed energies at the same geometry, no fitted parameter; the local-correlation thresholds are [10⁻⁷, 10⁻⁸] (decision 20). The two basis terms are common to the arm and to the canonical reference at each geometry and cancel in E_arm − E_canonical, so the smoothness and bias lines stand as printed; what they change is Δ₂ itself; the basis change of the CCSD(T)−MP2 remainder is not carried and is printed as the open term of the basis-set line (decision 26). Every arm reports the composite, and the bare LNO-CCSD(T) energy is kept beside it in the sealed file. The map is analytic while the overlaps are nonsingular (for |q| ≤ 1 the smallest singular value is 1 − O(q²)); for mode G the projection is inside the differentiated graph for both halves. **The three arms, written once:** **A** = frozen–frozen (the probe object: transported occupied set and transported LNO spaces); **B** = transported occupied set with fresh LNO spaces built on it (the released code as is, with the transported set passed as its localized-orbital input), so E(A) − E(B) is the virtual-freezing bias in isolation; **C** = fresh localiser and fresh LNO spaces (the production energy; on the D₆h rungs it carries the localiser's landing). Arm A needs one small override of the LNO-space construction to accept the stored, transported vectors — a subclass whose commit hash the deck pins. Q6's "without frozen spaces" arm is **B**, never C, so localiser arbitrariness is not attributed to freezing. **Probe M1** prints, along one totally symmetric, one degenerate and one non-symmetric benzene mode, per point: the continuity diagnostics (the smallest singular value of the occupied overlap S_oo(x) = C_occ(0)ᵀ S(x) C_occ(x) and the largest off-diagonal of the pre-Löwdin overlap, for both halves), E(A) − E(B) and E(A) − E(C), and for arm C the localiser's functional value and its overlap with the transported set — all without a verdict; M1's raw displaced energies go to the same hashed, sealed file as the R1 fit coefficients. Measured (Budget §3.1, `Research_Note_2026-09-05_Probe_M1.md` and the cc-pVTZ scans): the object round-trips and reloads exactly; arm A is smooth to 0.002–0.06 µE_h where the re-selecting arms carry 7–11 µE_h at default and 0.9–2.7 µE_h at tight thresholds; its frequency bias against canonical CCSD(T) is +0.11 / −0.01 / +0.23 cm⁻¹ at the anchor thresholds. **"Sealed" means:** the file is committed in plain text and readable in the public repository; its SHA-256 sidecar proves it was not altered; and the author has undertaken not to open it before the pilot note — a tamper seal and a discipline, not secrecy. A code that cannot freeze spaces is reported under stop 1.
- **Probe M1-μ, the frozen-space dipole (decision 18).** Along the three M1 benzene modes and the same nine points, arm A's dipole moment μ(q) — from the relaxed CC density where the code provides it, else by finite field in the frozen spaces (the field a deck number) — against the canonical CCSD(T) dipole at the same geometries; printed: σ of μ(q) about a degree-4 fit per component and the bias of ∂μ/∂q at q = 0 against canonical, in km/mol on that mode's intensity; the same for arm B. - **The structural prior is the symmetry prior.** Off-diagonal Δ₂ elements between DFT modes of **different irreducible representations** are fixed at zero (exact by symmetry, not an assumption); elements between modes of the **same** representation are free, whatever their frequency distance; an ℓ₁ penalty and a low-rank term remain only for rungs whose point group leaves the same-representation block too large to determine at K_cap (printed per rung: the number of free elements against the deck's off-diagonal count). Parameter-free where it applies. The representation of each DFT mode is determined by the deck's own symmetry analysis in the molecule's **full** point group (DFT codes run in Abelian subgroups and their labels would leave many symmetry-forbidden couplings free); degenerate pairs share one representation; a mode the analysis cannot assign is treated as coupling to all — never guessed. **Condition:** the symmetry prior enters the Q0 deck only after the **naphthalene dry run** (D₂h, eight representations, no degeneracy) reproduces the direct DFT−DFT Δ₂ within τ₇ on every family with it; until that test has printed, the frequency-banded prior stands as the deck's rule (band width w = the smallest width at which the dry run's full recovery reproduces the direct Δ₂ within τ₇ on every family; the ℓ₁ and low-rank weights by the dry run's held-out ρ minimum on the noise-injected column). The reason the band was retired at benzene: the large off-diagonal elements lay between modes 170–450 cm⁻¹ apart and all within one representation. The dry-run pair is B3LYP against a functional with markedly more exact exchange (BHLYP-class), never two functionals of one family. **Silent-only coupling blocks (decision 19):** modes that are IR-inactive by symmetry couple to IR-active modes only within their own representation and reach the spectrum only through anharmonic resonances; per rung the deck prints the number of same-representation pairs made of inactive modes only, and the rung's DFT dry run runs the recovery with and without those blocks and reports the scored bands' positions after GVPT2 for both; dropping the blocks is a deck decision for that rung only if every scored family's position moves by less than τ₇ in the dry run; default: keep. The diagonal of every mode (2 energies) is always measured.

## 5. Stop conditions and escalation (declared in advance)

1. **Probe M1 fails — no code can freeze spaces at the anchor level — or the anchor code is otherwise unavailable, or the B2 laptop underperforms:** the rung stops; the missing binary, option or measurement is named. Do not substitute a different level, or unfrozen spaces, and keep the rung's name. (M1 has passed at benzene; the stop remains armed for every later rung.)
2. **A rung crosses a machine checkpoint:** a written decision is mandatory — continue knowingly, reroute to B3, or stop. Silent overrun is forbidden, and so is ducking under a checkpoint by coarsening the basis, loosening thresholds, dropping CPS once mandatory, raising c, raising K_cap, enlarging q_s beyond the Q6 line, or dropping patterns. Human hours are never a stop condition.
3. **Cluster or rented-GPU access not formalised when first needed:** reach rungs stop and the stop is reported.
4. **A licence probe breaches its fixed threshold** — Q6 (noise line of the mode used at the rung's size class; bias line; threshold line without CPS), Q7 (Δ₂ outside τ₇, or the discriminability clause failed, or the shuffled-probe null passed, or recovered and direct couplings disagree beyond η₈·S on a resolved pair), Q8(a/b) on direct couplings (no locality, or a family's correction carried by long-range pairs), Q8(c) (no saturation), the learned-prior licence (prior-assisted vs structural beyond τ₇), the fragment licence (any of (a), (b), (b′), (c)), or ρ not reaching ρ\* by K_cap: a measured result, reported as such. Q6-noise breach at a size class: that mode carries no "beat" language there; the rung continues as a cost record and, if the other mode or CPS is licensed, under that. Q6-bias or Q7 or K_cap breach: Δ₂ does not enter a scored spectrum on the affected families at that rung; the pre-declared fallback is **DFT harmonic + DFT anharmonic, with Δ₂ applied only on families where Q7 passed, labelled per family** — it competes under the same protocol and may lose. Q8(a/b) or fragment-licence breach on a family: that family's Δ₂ is reported with its long-range share and carries no accuracy claim finer than that share; at R6 the family is withdrawn from the certificate. Learned-prior licence breach at R2 or R3: the prior is not spent on any rung. **Q8(c) breach: no size sentence** — the plan does not fall back to a point factory whose affordability no plan has measured.
5. **A promised accuracy rung loses to a line:** published with the paired table.

## 6. What this ladder refuses

- No global quartic force field of a huge molecule as a deliverable.
- No whole-molecule "gold rung" language above R1; anchors are "local-CC, R1-checked".
- **No motif-transfer claim:** every molecule gets its own probed Δ₂ — or, at R6, a fragment-probed Δ₂ under the fragment licence, labelled as such (a method decided by measurement, not a transfer of spectra). This refusal admits one *test*, gate **Q9** (decision 27; `notes/Research_Note_2026-09-08_P19_Transferability.md`): for each band family F (the C–H out-of-plane bands per hydrogen-adjacency class), a constant c_F or a one-parameter size law c_F + d_F/N_C, fitted leave-one-molecule-out over the eight ladder molecules plus the anthracene locality probe, wins only if the transfer error e_F(M) ≤ τ_F and the within-family spread s_F(M) ≤ τ_F on every R2 and R3 molecule, with τ_F the family's beat margin of §4 item 2; a family that loses is never applied above R3, and the losing sentence is pre-written. What a winning family passes upward: line A's positions shifted by c_F with the leave-one-out error bar, shown with provenance (no truth there), and one falsifiable prediction per family that the fragment-probed R6 checks.
