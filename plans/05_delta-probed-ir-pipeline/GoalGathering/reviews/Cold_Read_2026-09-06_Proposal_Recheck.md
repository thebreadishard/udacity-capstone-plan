## C. New problems introduced or exposed by the revision

Read once more start to finish. Each item: quoted text, what is wrong, proposed fix, class.

**C1. The benzene mode at 1357 cm⁻¹ is labelled two incompatible ways.** §3.3: "one component of a degenerate C–C stretch at 1357 cm⁻¹"; §3.2: "the strongest pair at 1186 and 1357 cm⁻¹ (B3LYP/6-31G* harmonic values) — and every one of them lies within a single irreducible representation". If 1357 belongs to a degenerate irrep, 1186 must be in the same degenerate irrep. Benzene's D₆h assignments (at B3LYP/6-31G* unscaled values as much as experimentally) offer no degenerate irrep with modes at both ~1186 and ~1357: e₂g has ~1200 and ~1640, e₁u ~1060 and ~1520. The non-degenerate b₂u pair (ν₁₅ ~1180, ν₁₄ Kekulé ~1350) fits both numbers, and the mode at 865 cm⁻¹, called "a C–H out-of-plane mode", is the one that is degenerate (e₁g). So either the word "degenerate" is attached to the wrong mode in §3.3, or the same-irrep statement in §3.2 — the single measured fact on which the symmetry prior (decision 11) rests — is wrong. A supervisor will stop here. Fix: print the irrep label beside each quoted frequency (1020, 1186, 1357, 865) in §3.2 and §3.3, taken from the DFT program's own labels, and correct whichever sentence is wrong. **BLOCKING** (trivially fixable if it is only a mislabel, but it must be checked, because the headline evidence for the prior depends on it).

**C2. K is defined but never stated for benzene, and its relation to the held-out energies is unclear.** Terms: "K is the number of coupled-cluster energies a molecule needed; K_off the part of it spent on the off-diagonal block." §3.4: "K is the number of energies at which the recovery's held-out residual ... first falls below a threshold." §8 gives 546 training, 122 held out, threshold reached at 448, K_off = 388 — but never K. The held-out energies must be computed to evaluate the residual, so they are energies the molecule "needed"; is K 448 or 570? The cost record's headline number is ambiguous by a quarter. Fix: in Terms, "K counts every coupled-cluster energy computed for the recovery, held-out energies included"; in §8, "K = ... at benzene". **MINOR** (but it is the one number the cost story is about).

**C3. The diagonal's cost is stated as two energies per mode, but the design uses four.** §5.1 step 2: "their diagonal costs two energies each"; §3.4: "The single-mode patterns are run at two amplitudes"; §8: 30 single-mode ± pairs (60 energies) "plus the 30 single-mode patterns at a second amplitude (60 energies)". That is four energies per mode. §4's "at least two energies per vibrational mode" survives as a lower bound, but §5.1's statement is simply wrong under the plan's own design. Fix: "four energies each (a ± pair at two amplitudes)". **MINOR.**

**C4. "Inside the laptop's budget" at naphthalene is asserted, not computed, and is conditional on a prior not yet admitted.** §3.2: "That difference is what puts the energy route at naphthalene inside the laptop's budget rather than on a cluster." The document's own figures: 2,087 s per local LNO-CCSD(T) energy at benzene/cc-pVTZ (§8), the licence rungs at cc-pVTZ, 48 modes × 4 diagonal energies + ~0.9 × 141 off-diagonal ≈ 320 energies at naphthalene, each slower than benzene's — i.e. of order 200–600 h before any held-out or reference energies, against the 168-hour line §8 uses to classify a batch as cluster work. That may still be acceptable on a dedicated machine, but the sentence reads as arithmetic and none is shown; and the prior it relies on "enters the deck after the naphthalene rehearsal reproduces the direct surrogate correction with it", which has not run. Fix: "would put ... inside the laptop's budget (estimate: N energies × t s ≈ H hours, laptop rule 168 h/batch), if the naphthalene rehearsal admits the prior". **MINOR.**

**C5. The cc-pVTZ scan's per-geometry time does not obviously match the per-energy times.** §3.3/§8: "about two hours per geometry"; §8: local LNO-CCSD(T) energy at cc-pVTZ 2,087 s, canonical 755 s. Presumably the scan runs fewer arms or one threshold; the text does not say what it comprises, so the reader cannot reconcile the numbers and cannot tell which of the M1 results (arms, thresholds, bias, noise) will exist at cc-pVTZ on Tuesday. Fix: "(arm A and arm C at tight thresholds plus the canonical point, ~2 h per geometry)" or whatever the composition is; also say which arm and threshold the 2,087 s figure refers to. **MINOR.**

**C6. The memory-scaling sentence contradicts its own exponent.** §8: "its memory scales roughly with the fourth power of the basis size, so of order ten times more would be needed" than 13.9 GB. Benzene has 114 functions in cc-pVDZ and 264 in cc-pVTZ; (264/114)⁴ ≈ 29, i.e. ~400 GB, not "of order ten times". Either the exponent or the multiplier is wrong; a computational chemist will do this multiplication in her head. Fix: "roughly thirty times (264/114)⁴ — hundreds of GB" or drop the estimate and keep only "did not complete within the 22 GB ceiling". **MINOR.**

**C7. "Deck number" misused, and the pilot note's closed input list is contradicted one section earlier.** §5.1 step 1: "The production DFT level ... is a deck number fixed in the pilot note and not yet chosen ... ## D. Closing judgment

Reading it once, I now know what is proposed (a per-molecule DFT harmonic/anharmonic treatment whose harmonic force constants are corrected by a probed LNO-CCSD(T)-minus-DFT difference, recovered in the DFT normal-mode basis under a symmetry prior, scored per band against three named opponents up a six-rung ladder), what has been measured (a DFT-only rehearsal at benzene whose arithmetic now closes, a 27-point frozen-space smoothness and bias scan at cc-pVDZ with a canonical truth line, and the timings that put the canonical diagonal line on the laptop and the canonical Hessian and gradient off it), and what is promised (positions wherever a band is decidable, intensities on two molecules, a fragment-probed spectrum or a documented refusal at the top, cost records in numbers only). What is asked of me is explicit and answerable from the document for four of the five requests; the fifth (the evaluation contract, §13.1) is answerable in structure but not in detail, because two opponents still lack a checkable reference and the DFT level that defines the corrected object is deferred to the pilot note. The one thing I would not sign off on without a reply is C1: the 1357 cm⁻¹ mode cannot be both degenerate and the same-irrep partner of 1186 cm⁻¹, and since the symmetry prior is justified by that pair, the irrep labels must be printed before the prior is called "what symmetry gives for free".
