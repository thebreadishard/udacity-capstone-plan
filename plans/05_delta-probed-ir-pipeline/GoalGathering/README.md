## `notes/` — research notes, side project, requests, hardware

Dated working notes that record a measurement or a decision input: `Research_Note_*`, `Side_Project_*`,
`PDF_Request_*`, `Hardware_Note_*`. They are cited from the binding documents by dated note; they are not
themselves binding.

## `reviews/` — the review record

`Professor_Review_*` (Rounds 7–10, Pass A/B), their `Review_Brief_*`, the `Seam_Check_*` of the Round-10B
patch, and the `Cold_Read_*` reports (literature 2026-09-06; proposal 2026-09-06, -08, -10 with rechecks).
The record is complete and closed; new reviews go here.

The rule for anything new: a measurement or decision → `notes/`; a review or cold read → `reviews/`; a
change to a binding document → a dated note inside that document, never a new top-level file.
