## What plan 06 is

A side study, opened on 12 September 2026, asking one question in two forms about the project's expensive step — the coupled-cluster correction Δ₂ to a DFT Hessian, measured as a deck of local-CC energies (K = 448 at benzene, ≈ 474 at naphthalene): **(M) is there a different mathematics that gives the same correction within the project's error budget, and (C) can the deck be made cheap enough that the pipeline runs many times?** It works only on data the main plan already has (the sealed benzene dry-run tensor, stored DFT Hessians of nine PAHs, run logs) and on desk mathematics; anything it finds enters the main plan only through that plan's dated decision record. Two such transfers exist so far (decision 34; a second, P25, pending a pre-registered test).

## What has been established (all measured on the benzene stand-in tensor unless stated)

1. **The correction is a symmetry-blocked, nearly dense object, and it is not local.** It has full rank (30 of 30); only 47 of its 435 off-diagonal mode pairs are symmetry-allowed; in real space its far atom-pair blocks are small in norm but not negligible for band positions — keeping 99 % of the Frobenius norm still moves bands by 14 cm⁻¹, and 74 of 78 atom-pair blocks are needed for 0.5 cm⁻¹ [X8]. Its share of the DFT Hessian *rises* with bond-graph distance (2.7 % on-atom → 6.5 % at three bonds; 19–26 % for ring C–C pairs) [X9]: the correction follows the π system, not the bonds. For the DFT Hessians of nine PAHs the far blocks are low-rank in norm (as the 2025 O1NumHess method assumes) but need 30–83 % of their rank at 0.5 cm⁻¹ [X15]. A method rule follows: sparsity or low rank must be tested on band positions, never on norms.
2. **Exact recovery from few Hessian–vector products.** Powell–Toint substitution on the symmetry pattern recovers Δ₂ exactly (to 10⁻¹⁶) from 6–7 products at benzene and **9 products at naphthalene** — the size of the largest irreducible-representation block — i.e. 18 gradients against the 474-energy deck [X1c, X14]; the recovery theorems are machine-checked in Lean 4/Mathlib (no `sorry`). Measurement noise is magnified 1.4–1.5× [X1d]. With energies only, the products cost more than the deck; the route pays only with an analytic or AD gradient, and pays as soon as one gradient costs fewer than ≈ 26 energies at naphthalene.
3. **A free DFT ordering of the deck.** Ranking the symmetry-allowed pairs by the resonance denominator 1/|ω_i² − ω_j²| — the standard VPT2 resonance criterion, applied here to which harmonic couplings to measure first — selects 19 of 47 pairs for 0.5 cm⁻¹ at benzene (a perfect element-size prior: 17) [X10]; the substitution count on that pattern drops from 7 to 4 [X11]. Proposed to the main plan as an *ordering* the existing stopping rule judges, never a truncation (P25); its licence test is the naphthalene repeat, pre-registered.
4. **The one unmeasured number.** Every gradient-based saving hangs on g, the cost of one local-CC gradient in units of one energy. No paper prints it (the automatic-differentiation literature bounds it by a small constant; Zhang et al. 2024 demonstrate LNO-CCSD(T) gradients by AD without a ratio). Its measurement is pre-registered (M2a: cells, timing rule, thresholds g ≤ 6 / ≤ 20 / > 20 fixed before any run; PySCFAD 0.3.3 installed) and runs after the current naphthalene timing.

## What is and is not new

Every ingredient exists: nearsightedness and its decay theorems (Prodan & Kohn 2005; Benzi, Boito & Razouk 2013), sparse-Hessian recovery by graph colouring (Curtis–Powell–Reid 1974; Powell & Toint 1979; Coleman & Moré 1984), compressed-sensing Hessians of polyacenes (Sanders, Andrade & Aspuru-Guzik 2015), O(1)-gradient Hessians by off-diagonal low rank (Wang, Luo, Wang & Liu 2025), AD gradients of local CC (Zhang et al. 2024), the VPT2 resonance criterion. A citation chase over 873 works citing seventeen of these seeds (OpenAlex, 13 September) finds the colouring literature never entering chemistry, and no coupled-cluster correction to a PAH Hessian recovered from few local-CC measurements. Plan 06's own piece is the combination around that object, measured, with the band-level test and the finding that the correction is the long-range part of the problem. It has produced no new solver.
